# Blog: Technical Overview of Seedance 2.0 (Apr 2, 2026)

Sources:
- https://higgsfield.ai/blog/Seedance-2.0-AI-Video-Technical-Preview

Editorial, no prompts, no hands-on numbers. Existing Seedance notes: [../../features/models/seedance.md](../../features/models/seedance.md), [../../blog/generating-with-seedance-2-0.md](../../blog/generating-with-seedance-2-0.md).

- Availability note at the time: Seedance 2.0 on Higgsfield required **business email verification** and was offered in all regions **except the US and Japan**.
- The article frames Seedance 2.0 as an incremental step beyond Seedance 1.5 whose value is **sequence-level coherence** rather than single pretty frames: stable motion, consistent framing, deliberate camera logic (pans, tracking shots, controlled reveals), lighting/texture stability across long or slow shots, persistent character identity, and physically plausible fine motion in macro shots.
- Practical reading: judge a video model on long slow takes and macro product shots (they expose drift), and on whether output survives editing and compositing - that is the test the article says creators now apply.
