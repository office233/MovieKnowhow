## Notes

**Topic:** Kling Motion Control 3.0 (David Matamoros, Mar 5 2026; day-0 on Higgsfield). No prompts.
- What: character image + motion reference video -> the character performs the video's movements, facial expressions and pacing, identity preserved. Real motion is the "source of truth" instead of text. 3.0 vs 2.6: better facial consistency, tighter capture, better reference alignment (esp. complex moves and facial close-ups).
- **Steps:** Video tab -> Kling Motion Control 3.0 -> upload motion reference video -> upload character photo with clearly visible face and body -> quality 720p/1080p (1080p recommended) -> **Scene source**: background from the motion video or from the character image -> Advanced Settings: optional text prompt for background/lighting/atmosphere; orientation matching video or image -> Generate.
- Best motion refs: single subject, clear unobstructed movement, good lighting, clean background; dance and gesture clips work best.
- Uses: animate AI characters/concept art without keyframing; viral trend dances on custom characters (one motion template reused across many characters/styles = content series); brand mascots; AI influencers (one character asset, many motions).
