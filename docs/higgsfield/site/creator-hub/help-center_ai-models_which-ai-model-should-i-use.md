# Which AI model should I use?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/ai-models/which-ai-model-should-i-use
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (5 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (model chooser). Pick a family; Higgsfield uses its latest version automatically.

**Images**: Soul (fashion/cinematic, character consistency via Soul ID; start here if one consistent character matters) | Popcorn (storyboards, multi-frame) | Nano Banana (speed + strong reference control/swapping; products, characters) | GPT Image (readable text, true color) | Seedream (complex, compositional prompts; visual reasoning) | Recraft (photoreal or vector) | FLUX (speed-optimized detail) | Upscale tool.
**Video**: Higgsfield DoP (VFX + directed camera presets; "when camera work is the point") | Seedance (realistic human motion, expressions; most forgiving for beginners) | Kling (longer clips, multi-shot continuity, physical motion, motion transfer) | Wan (precise first/last frame control; continuing scenes) | Grok Imagine (synced audio) | Sora (OpenAI) | Veo (Google) | MiniMax Hailuo (high-dynamic, fast).
**Audio**: Seed Audio (full sound scene: multi-speaker dialogue, music, SFX in one pass; also TTS, cloning, dubbing) | Eleven (emotional nuance) | MiniMax Speech (studio TTS) | Seed Speech (multilingual) | VibeVoice (long-form).
**Models vs tools**: Cinema Studio, Canvas, Marketing Studio, Supercomputer are tools that host models; Cinema Studio can switch video families per scene. Beginners: Soul or Nano Banana for images, Seedance for video. Cost varies by family, resolution, duration; availability depends on plan.
