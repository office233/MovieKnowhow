## Notes

**Topic:** "All Unlimited" offer explained (Jul 22 2026). No prompts.

**Two layers:** (1) plan-included **365-day unlimited sets** (plans above entry tier; lasts 365 days from activation, no rollover/renewal, needs active subscription); (2) **All Unlimited** time-boxed window (1-7 days) on 23 models: 11 image (Nano Banana Pro/2/1, Seedream 5.0 Pro/Lite, Seedream 4.5, GPT Image 2, GPT Image, Kling O1, Soul 2.0, FLUX.2 Pro — up to 2K), 7 video (Seedance 2.0 / Fast / Mini 720p 8 s; Kling 3.0 + Motion Control 1080p 8 s; Gemini Omni Flash 7 s; Wan 2.7 1080p 8 s — up to 15 s on higher tiers/annual/bundle), 5 audio (Seed Audio 1.0, Eleven v3, MiniMax Speech 2.8 HD, VibeVoice, Seed Speech).

**Routes:** 1-day free trial for new users (auto-renews to Plus + 7 days unlimited); Plus ($49/mo) and higher include 7 days on purchase (Starter/Basic don't); **Unlimited Bundle** in the Marketplace: 1 day $99 or 3 days $250, video up to 15 s regardless of plan. Comparison: buying 1 day individually for Seedance 2.0 ($73) + Seedance Fast/Mini ($35) + Kling 3.0 ($35) + GPT Image 2 ($20) + Nano Banana Pro ($5) = $168 for 6 models.

**Real limits:**
- **1 concurrent generation per format**, shared across models (credit generations keep plan concurrency, up to 8 on high tiers).
- **Web app only** — MCP, CLI, Supercomputer, Canvas, plugins, and internal Presets/Apps/Studios always charge credits.
- Standard modes only (e.g., no 4K); standard queue — slower at peak. **Credit Mode** toggle = priority queue instantly.
- Fair use: personal human use; no automation/scripting/credential sharing/reselling.
- Needs an active subscription; window doesn't reset at renewal.

**Activation checklist:** switch the **Unlimited toggle** on in the model menu (most common mistake); check Manage Account -> Subscription -> Active Unlimited Models (start/expiry); offers on Pricing banner, models page, Marketplace (profile menu -> Unlimited Models).
