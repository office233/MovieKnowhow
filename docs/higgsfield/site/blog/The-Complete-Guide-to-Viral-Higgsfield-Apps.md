# The Complete Guide to Viral Higgsfield Apps

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/blog/The-Complete-Guide-to-Viral-Higgsfield-Apps  
Mariam Barova, Nov 21, 2025  
Prompts extracted: 0

Prompt-free one-photo "Apps" (built on Nano Banana Pro) — each mimics a viral visual trend. Generic flow: Apps -> choose app -> upload photo -> (optional) outfit refs -> preset -> Generate; for video-capable apps, then "Generate Video" and pick an animation style.

| App | Category | Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Style Snap | outfit try-on | img->img | 14 style presets (streetwear, vintage, minimal, editorial, techwear, Y2K…); keeps face/pose/light; best with mid-shots (close-up in = close-up out) |
| Outfit Vending | fashion/accessory | img->img | "fashion vending machine" — outfit pieces presented in a vending machine and overlaid realistically |
| Glitter Sticker | sticker sheet | img->img | kawaii glitter sticker pack, sparkles/pastels; K-pop/anime audiences |
| Signboard | billboard mockup | img->img | puts photo on realistic billboard/shop sign — musicians, album covers, brand tests |
| Paint App | redraw | img->img | turns photo into MS-Paint-style art; accepts stylized inputs |
| Nano Strike | Counter-Strike-style character | img->img & video | up to 5 people per image (group memes); video: character walks forward while explosion behind |
| Nano Theft | GTA-style poster | img->img & video | bold outlines/color blocks; multiple people; thumbnails; same walk+explosion animation |
| Simlife | SIMS-style avatar | img->img & video | use a white-background photo; up to 2 clothing reference photos; video of the Sim waving |
| Japanese Show | 90s–2000s Japanese variety TV | img->img | neon frames, bold type, game-show overlays |
