## Notes

**Topic:** AI UGC ads with Marketing Studio (Aug 20 2026).

**Pipeline:** product -> hook + script -> creator -> product in frame -> vertical export.
**Two principles:** (1) the **hook in the first 2 seconds** (unexpected object, bold claim, question, before-state); (2) the **micro-creator look** — imperfect framing, natural light, conversational delivery (polished = "ad").

**Genres:** GRWM (TikTok/Reels; "I replaced my entire morning routine with one product"), Tutorial ("You always used it wrong"), Before/after ("Day 1 vs day 30"), Testimonial (Meta feed; "I was skeptical until week two"), Unboxing ("The packaging alone sold me"), POV demo ("POV: your desk setup finally makes sense").

**Workflow:**
1. Product shots once per product (paste link or upload; Studio white for pack shot, Usage for context) — reuse across all ads.
2. Brand face: library avatar, avatar from a text prompt, or from a photo you have rights to (still adjustable by prompt).
3. Hook + script are separate from the preset (preset = look, script = words). Open on the line that earns 2 s; a claim that contradicts assumptions beats a description. **Ad Reference** rebuilds a permitted reference ad's structure around your product.
4. Batch up to **4 generations** and change **one variable**: for hook tests keep product/creator/preset/setting fixed, change only the opening line; test presets in a separate batch. Rank with **Virality Predictor**.
5. Review voiceover, pacing and product claims; export in the placement's aspect ratio.

**Controls:** 32 styles — Product shot (9), Ads (Social proof, Comparison, Problem->Solution), Marketplace (Dark, 3D surreal, Color pop, Pastel, Clean Studio), Motion (2D product motion, Hypermotion, Typography, Dark Minimalism, Color pop, Mixed media, SaaS), UGC video (Shopping, At home, Delivery, Review, Try-on, Unboxing, Tutorial, Before/after); 1,500+ presets; Avatar slot, Product slot, Ad Reference; 9 aspect ratios (auto, 1:1, 3:2, 2:3, 4:3, 3:4, 9:16, 16:9, 21:9); up to 15 s with native audio/lip sync.
**Scale:** keep winning creator + structure and swap product; Marketplace/Posters from same setup; Recreate to swap one input.
**Costs (~20 cr/$):** UGC video 52-142 cr ($2.60-7.10); product image 2-4 cr; poster/marketplace 8 cr.
**Disclosure:** TikTok requires labelling realistic AI content (synthetic creator/voice); Meta has an AI disclosure control in Ads Manager; strictest for political/social-issue ads.
