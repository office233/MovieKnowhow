## Notes

**Topic:** Similarity/Content Scoring for commercial workflows (Mar 16 2026), Team Plan feature. No prompts.

- Scans generated images/videos (and audio) **before delivery** for resemblance to known characters, celebrity likeness, logos/brands, franchise design assets, specific film frames, directorial styles, real people/biometrics, and music.
- Output per flag: **type** (character/brand/likeness/style), **reference source**, **location in the media** (where in frame/timeline), **percentage score**. It is guidance, **not a blocker** — you decide to publish or revise.
- Pipeline: content analysis (frames + audio) -> pattern matching -> score -> creator review.
- Detection categories: Actor Likeness, Brand/Trademark, Design Assets, Fictional Character, Film/Movie Frame, Cinematics & Direction, Aesthetic DNA, Real Person, Biometrics & Identity, Audio.
- Internal benchmark claim: video detection accuracy 86.6% vs 48.5% for a third-party tool; video false-positive rate 13.3% vs 73.8%.
- Practical use: run on ad/campaign assets before client submission to avoid costly post-launch likeness/IP conflicts.
