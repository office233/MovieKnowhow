# How do I use Cinema Studio?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/tools/how-do-i-use-cinema-studio
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (5 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (tool guide). Cinema Studio = simulated film studio: optics and camera motion control, Soul Cast AI actors, color grading, physics-aware motion with native audio, AI Director, Elements. Version 4.0 loads by default; selector switches 2.0-4.0.

| Version | Best for | Key capability |
|---|---|---|
| 2.5 | AI actors + grading | up to 3 Soul Cast characters; Colorgrade (temperature, contrast, saturation, sharpness, film grain, highlights, exposure); no video upload |
| 3.0 | realism, physics | up to 9 references; reasoning engine (no manual start/end frames per shot); genres; speed ramps; video upload up to 15 s |
| 3.5 | AI-assisted directing, teams | Claude Chat AI Director; Elements (Characters, Locations, Props); shared projects; video up to 15 s |
| 4.0 | deepest control, longest clips | runs on Seedance 2.5; Director's Panel; up to 50 refs; clips up to 30 s; video upload up to 30 s |

**3.0 settings**: Genre General/Action/Horror/Comedy/Noir/Drama/Epic; Speed ramp Linear/Auto/Flash In/Flash Out/Slow-mo/Bullet Time/Impact/Ramp Up; camera movements; native SFX/speech/music.
**3.5**: per-shot Camera, Lens, Focal length, Aperture; global Genre, Style, Lighting, Color Palette, Camera MoveSet Style (8 palettes, 10 MoveSets inspired by real cinematographers, 6 lighting presets). Claude can break a script into shots with camera params pre-filled; its prompts land in the prompt box for you to review; Claude never clicks Generate.
**4.0 Director's Panel**: Film Setup = genre + era (60s-2020s) + tempo (Single Shot ... Chaotic cut speed); Camera = body (35mm Film, 8mm Film, DV Camcorder, ...), lens (clean sharp to anamorphic/vintage), aperture; moves incl. POV, Robot Arm, Helicopter Shot; Color Palette 50+ templates; Lighting 6 presets or custom (color, brightness, diffusion, angle); character emotion slider (expressiveness). Modes: References (new clip), Forward/Backward Extend (continue an uploaded clip up to 30 s either direction), Edit Video (regional edit of object/face).
**Consistency**: save characters/locations/props as Elements (Soul Cast -> Save to Elements) and add them as references.
**Errors**: "May contain protected content" = real face or IP detected -> remove from prompt/refs. AI Director missing -> refresh / re-login. Unlimited applies only if Cinema Studio models are in your plan's Unlimited list.
