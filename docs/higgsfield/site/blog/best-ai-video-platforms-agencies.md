Agency needs: separate client projects, seats with pooled billing/roles/spend control, commercial rights that transfer to clients.
**Higgsfield business plans**: **Team** $69/seat/mo ($65 annual), 2–9 seats, 1,000 credits/seat/mo pooled, **16 parallel generations**, shared Assets; **Scale** $169/seat ($150 annual), 5–15 seats, 2,500 credits/seat, **32 parallel generations**, priority queue, SSO, pause member spend; **Enterprise**: sub-workspaces per client/brand/region with own credit allocation, roles/user groups (usage limits, model restrictions), SOC 2-aligned, GDPR, no data training, indemnification. **Terms of Use 4.4: you own outputs on every plan, commercial use unrestricted, rights survive cancellation and can be transferred/sublicensed to clients.** Unlimited applies on web only (MCP, CLI, Canvas, Supercomputer use credits).
Revision workflow: **Genjutsu** changes one element of a finished video (product, text, background) while preserving approved footage; dubbing + reframing cover language and vertical versions without new generation.
New-client checklist: create client space; folders by deliverable (ads, socials, drafts, approved); scoped roles, time-limited freelancer access; load brand assets/references; credit allocation/spend limits + reporting; note output ownership in the client contract.
Competitors: Runway Team $69/seat (6,900 credits/seat); HeyGen Business $149 + $20/seat; **Synthesia stock avatars not licensed for paid ads** (custom avatars needed); Luma per-member private workspaces; Adobe Firefly teams + IP indemnification for eligible features; Kling Team $79/seat (10,000 credits/seat, up to 5 workspaces/user, web only); Pika/Hailuo individual plans.
