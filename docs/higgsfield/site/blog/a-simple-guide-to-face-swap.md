# Your Simple Guide to the Next-Gen Face Swap & Its Best Use Cases

Mariam Barova, Oct 28, 2025

**Face Swap** (Create Image or Apps): identity embedding with lighting/shadow matching and geometry alignment; no prompt, mask or retouch.
Steps: open Face Swap -> upload **input image** (defines pose, light direction, framing; clear, well-lit face) -> upload **reference image** (the face to insert) -> Generate (30 s–2 min depending on plan/queue).
Limits: one face per operation (no multi-face swap). Free plan: 5 face swaps/day (resets ~24 h after first swap). Commercial rights on paid Pro plans. "Easy Export for Animation" sends results to video models.
Use-cases:
- Stylists: put a client's face on pre-shot model photos with 10 different haircuts/makeup looks.
- **Film/story continuity**: apply your main character's face across generated scenes (close-ups to action) to keep identity; digital doubles; hybrid human+AI production.
- Restoration: rebuild damaged faces in old photos using a clean photo of the same person; re-aging for documentaries.
