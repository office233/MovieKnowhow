# How do promo prices work?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/plans/how-do-promo-prices-work
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (2 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (plans). Promo price = one-time discount on first payment/stated period, then standard price. Sources: promocodes, Exclusive Offers. Offer bonuses (Unlimited, bonus credits, priority) are time-limited. Refunds: credit-type promos within 7 days if unused; access/feature offers (Unlimited period, upgrade, priority) non-refundable once granted.
