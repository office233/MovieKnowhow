# When does my subscription renew?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/plans/when-does-my-subscription-renew
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (3 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (plans). Stripe auto-renews on the purchase anniversary date (monthly or yearly); upgrade resets the date. Monthly credits reset when the invoice is paid; annual credits reset every 30 days. Renewal date: Manage Account -> Subscription ("Your plan renews [date]"); invoices under Upcoming Invoice -> All invoices. Failed renewal = past due; about 14 days to fix before cancellation.
