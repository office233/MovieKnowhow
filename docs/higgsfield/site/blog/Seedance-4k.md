# How to Create Ultra-Realistic Videos with Seedance 4K: Guide with Ready-To-Use Prompts

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/blog/Seedance-4k  
Mariam Barova, Jun 25, 2026 (companion to YouTube "Seedance 4K Just Made AI Filmmaking Look Ultra Realistic!")  
Prompts extracted: 16

Stress test of **Seedance 2.0 native 4K** across four formats, with every prompt.
- **Cinematic** (dragon oner, sea-kaiju underwater battle, ice titan vs army "impossible shot"): 4K keeps scale detail — dragon scales, spray/foam layers, individual soldiers in wide crowds don't melt into "pixel soup".
- **Real footage + AI (V2V)**: record on a phone, reference it as `@video_1`, lock "subject, face, pose, office, camera and motion reference — preserve exactly", then transform: creature FX, **hand -> robot hand**, object removal, material changes (metal, lava, ice, crystal, energy). Replaces masking/roto/tracking.
- **Animation**: surreal painterly fantasy flight; first-person alien POV with **speed-ramp into macro slow motion and back**; multi-shot Pixar-style 3D (you don't need to prompt every cut — "montage, multi-shot… don't use one camera angle" lets the model stage it); FPV oner through gears and balloons. Seedance outputs **10-bit color** (smoother gradients in smoke/reds).
- **Ads**: hypermotion CGI product spot ("about 15sec, 10 scenes / cuts", tagging an element `@Supercomputer-Heroine`) — text renders sharper in 4K; luxury skincare ad with multiple character references, per-character colour worlds, one FILM BURN transition — realistic skin texture avoids uncanny valley.
- **Games**: UE5-style open-world cutscene with live HUD matched to `@image_1` (image-to-video); arcade racer gameplay; third-person boss fight oner with persistent HUD.
Tips: describe your idea to Claude with the **prompt-builder skill** (downloadable on page) to get production-ready prompts. **Image model pairing**: Soul Cinema for characters/locations/keyframes (8 batches for 1 credit, cinematic by default); **GPT Image 2 when you need text, motion or storyboards**.
