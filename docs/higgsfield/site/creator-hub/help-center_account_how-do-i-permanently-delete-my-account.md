# How do I permanently delete my account?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/account/how-do-i-permanently-delete-my-account
Published: Aug 2, 2026 (3 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (account). Self-service: Manage Account -> Personal Profile -> Manage Account Deletion -> confirm checklist -> Delete Account. Subscription auto-canceled; all generations, trained Soul characters, presets, credits lost. 30-day recovery window (sign back in to restore), then permanent. Download assets first. Content already used for training cannot be removed from trained models; deleted content not used going forward.
