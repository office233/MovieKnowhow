## Notes

**Topic:** building a food commercial shot by shot (Aug 26 2026).

**Shot types & jobs:** Hero shot = payoff (open/final reveal); Macro = texture/appetite; Splash/pour = motion + freshness (hook or transition); Steam/heat = hot and just made; Slow motion = emphasis on one impact moment; Ingredient motion = process before payoff. Small details (drizzle, steam, condensation) sell more than a static wide of the dish.

**Workflow (Marketing Studio):**
1. Upload a clear product photo or paste the product URL (packaging/colour/brand reference).
2. Define goal first: hook, benefit/offer, CTA, placement. Short social ad = strong opening + 2-3 appetite shots + product + CTA.
3. Choose the shot types that goal needs.
4. Pick a format — **Product Showcase** (clean packaging reveal), **Hyper Motion** (splashes, fast ingredient motion), **TV Spot** (cinematic multi-shot), **UGC** (creator recommendation) — or prompt a custom shot (example below: self-assembling burger on #000000).
5. Generate each shot. 6. Review accuracy (product, colours, intentional motion). 7. Assemble best takes: hook -> appetite -> payoff -> CTA; add audio/copy; export. Typical ad = **3-5 shots**. Use **Brand Kit** for brand colours/fonts across the campaign.

**Prompting tips:** name the exact texture ("glossy caramel drizzle"); give motion a direction and speed ("sauce pouring slowly from top left"); explicitly call out steam/condensation/moisture (rarely appears unless named); specify distance ("extreme close-up" vs "close-up"); describe lighting identically in every shot for continuity; reference the real product photo when labels/colours must match. Food looking "off" usually = a missing texture or light detail in the prompt.
**Format adaptation:** social = fast hook + 1-2 detail shots + reveal; product page = more packaging/texture; vertical = compose pours/splashes for 9:16 from the start (don't crop later); marketplace = clean uncluttered product; brand campaign = more cinematic story. Aspect: 9:16 Stories/Reels, 1:1 feed, 16:9 YouTube/landing page.
