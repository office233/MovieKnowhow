# How do Higgsfield plans work?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/plans/how-do-higgsfield-plans-work
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (3 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (plans). Individual plans (solo creators) differ by model access (entry = core models, higher = all incl. newest), monthly credits, parallel generations, and Unlimited models (mid/top). Monthly or annual (annual cheaper per month). Business plans: Team, Scale (more credits/seat, priority queue, SSO, spend controls), Enterprise (custom volume, rollover credits, dedicated capacity, compliance, user groups). Free usage = small model set, no credits, watermark. Pricing page has an interactive plan picker and comparison table.
