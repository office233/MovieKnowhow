## Notes

**Topic:** Click to Ad — product URL to video ad, for 100+ creatives/month solo (Jun 21 2026). No prompts.
- Paste any single-product page URL -> extracts **product name, description, up to 8 photos, brand colours, logo** -> you review/replace anything -> choose one of **10 templates** (minimal/text-forward to dynamic/brand-focused) -> generate; re-run with other templates for platform variants/A-B tests.
- Best inputs: at least one clear product photo (no photo = no ad); **product-only photos beat photos with people** (models get weighted over the product); physical goods with clear shapes (electronics, clothing, accessories, skincare, packaged food, home goods); clean galleries (icons/thumbnails get pulled in otherwise); clear title/description.
- Limits: template-based (bespoke/narrative ads -> Marketing Studio / Cinema Studio); cluttered pages need manual fixes; weaker for digital/services.
- Use cases: e-commerce launches, small businesses without a video team, A/B tests, agencies drafting from client sites, catalog/seasonal refreshes.
- Competitors: Creatify (UGC avatars), Pippit by CapCut, AdCreative.ai. Plans: Starter $15 (200 cr), Plus $49 (1,000 cr; $39 annual).
