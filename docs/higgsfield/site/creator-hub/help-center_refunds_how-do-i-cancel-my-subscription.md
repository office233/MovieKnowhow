# How do I cancel my subscription?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/refunds/how-do-i-cancel-my-subscription
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (2 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (refunds). Cancel = turn off auto-renewal: Manage Account -> Subscription -> Plan Settings -> Manage Subscription -> Show -> Cancel Subscription. Access continues to period end, then free tier; no automatic refund; account and generations kept. Team/Scale/Enterprise canceled by the workspace Owner. Credits after cancel: subscription credits expire at period end; Pack/Auto-Refill/Boost (90 days) and promo credits stay usable on free-plan models.
