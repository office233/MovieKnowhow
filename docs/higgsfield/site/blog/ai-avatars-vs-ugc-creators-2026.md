# AI Avatars vs UGC Creators for Ads in 2026: Which Performs Better

Decision table: genuine testimonial/hands-on demo -> human UGC; existing audience/distribution -> influencer; fast hook/script testing, multiple languages, many controlled variations with one spokesperson -> **AI avatar**; paid-social performance -> test both under identical conditions; trust + scale -> **human master ad + AI variations**.
Costs: AI avatar in Marketing Studio — 8 s 720p ≈ 47.62 credits (~$2.38); 15 s 720p ≈ 89.2 credits (~$4.46) (Sep 2026), plus retries. Human UGC benchmark $150–$300+ per short video, plus scripting, shipping, revisions, raw footage, paid usage rights, whitelisting, exclusivity.
Time: AI avatar ad hours–1 day; UGC ad days–2 weeks.
AI avatar workflow in Marketing Studio (1,500+ templates): choose/create avatar -> Soul ID for cross-campaign consistency -> add product via image or URL -> select format -> generate and review script, product details, voice, lip-sync, consistency -> regenerate/variations. Small product details/text often need regeneration.
Cases: Lu do Magalu (virtual creator), Lil Miquela (virtual influencer), **Brilliance × Higgsfield** jewelry content: ~80% lower production cost (~$100k -> ~$10k + labor), 5–10x more output, turnaround 6–8 -> 3–4 days.
Testing plan: AI avatar to test 5 hooks x 4 languages (measure hook-level CTR, cost per test) -> bring a human creator in for the winning concept if authenticity matters. Measure CTR, completion rate, CPA, ROAS. Check platform AI-labeling rules; avatars can't give genuine testimonials.
