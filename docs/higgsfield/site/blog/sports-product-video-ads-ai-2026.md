## Notes

**Topic:** sports product photos -> video ads (Sep 2026). No prompts, but hook templates.
- Why motion: sports products prove themselves in motion (sole flex under a step, fabric moving with the body, equipment doing its job) — grip, flex, fit, impact, recovery don't read in a still.
- Prep: product photos from multiple angles incl. a material/texture close-up; a motion or athlete reference if available; target platform.
- **Workflow A — Marketing Studio (fast):** Click to Ad URL or upload photos (auto name, description, up to 8 photos, colours, logo) -> high-motion / product-in-action template (1,500+ templates, 6 categories) -> add a motion reference clip -> generate -> adjust hook, text, CTA -> export.
- **Workflow B — Cinema Studio 4.0 (camera control, up to 30 s):** product photo -> set genre, lens, camera move directly (tracking on a stride, low-angle push-in on a footstrike) -> tempo, grade, era -> motion reference -> generate -> bring into Marketing Studio for hook/text/CTA.
- Spokesperson: Soul ID (face) + Lipsync Studio (lip sync) + a separate consistent voice preset/custom voice — three separate layers. Only with permission; don't imply real-athlete endorsement.
- **Hook formulas:** "See how [product] handles [specific movement]." · "[Feature] built for [specific condition]." · "Watch the [sole/material/grip] respond during [action]." · "From [problem] to [result] in one movement." · "[Product] tested against [condition], not just described." · "The [feature] that changes how [product] performs during [action]."
- Pricing (15 s): Marketing Studio product video 90 cr ($4.50), UGC 108 cr ($5.40); Cinema Studio 4.0 motion/camera shot 135 cr ($6.75).
- Launch checklist: logo visible on product; motion readable on a paused frame; price + CTA in final seconds; vertical for Reels/TikTok; length fits platform; test hook variations before scaling spend.
