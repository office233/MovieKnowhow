# AI Face and Character Swap in Video & Photo: PRO Guide from Top Engineer

Face Swap replaces only the face (needs matched face shape, skin tone, angle — often needs masking and multiple passes). **Character Swap** (Instadump for images; Character Video Swap) transfers the whole character — face form, skin color, emotion, body.

**Higgsfield Recast** (character swap in video): high realism, audio/video sync, background change, dubbing, voice cloning, 30+ character presets + background presets, own reference characters/voices.
- Voice cloning: upload a clean excerpt (quiet room, no music/echo) or pick character voices; dubbing can translate AND change voice gender while keeping tone/intonation.
- Backgrounds: Cozy Minimalist (expert/work content), Pink Room (lifestyle/UGC), Luxury Masculine (high-status), Green Screen (replace with any image/video).
- "Recast a Movie": insert your cloned character into a movie scene or trailer.

Pro tips for believable swaps:
1. **One person in frame.** With multiple faces the model swaps the one closest to camera — simplify, frame the main character, remove/blur others.
2. **Avoid pure white backgrounds** in the input image — no depth cues, worse blending; use a soft-colored or textured wall/room.
3. **Empty hands** — held props (cup, bag, phone) are read as body geometry and get projected into the result.
4. **Match color palette/lighting** between source photo and reference video (warm vs neon mismatch breaks skin texture).
5. **Film frontal, not side-on**; slow continuous motion (walking, sitting, looking around), no fast head turns/acrobatics; sit on a low-back chair to keep a clean outline.
6. **Face clearly visible in the first frame** — no hands/mics over face, no extreme contortions.
7. **Match body proportions** — very different builds distort.
Uses: replace actors across scenes with one Soul ID, A/B ad variants, localize campaigns with different ambassadors.
