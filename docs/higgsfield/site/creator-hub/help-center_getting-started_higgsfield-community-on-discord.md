# How do I join the Higgsfield community on Discord?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/getting-started/higgsfield-community-on-discord
Published: Jul 30, 2026 (7 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (community). Generations are private by default; Publish from Assets (three-dot menu) to show on higgsfield.ai/@username; popular works can be featured on Explore. Turn off "Auto-publish new generations" in Manage Account -> Personal Profile to stay private.

Official Discord: channels for rules/help-center, announcements/updates/community-events/streams, #prompt-discussion, #tutorials, #learning-hub (breakdowns, prompts, templates), #your-creations, #collaborations, #daily-soul, #hall-of-fame, #monthly-awards, #support, #community-support, #bug-reports, #suggestions. #verification links your plan and unlocks a verified hub. Roles: Team, Moderators, Ambassadors, Power Users (selected, no application), Bug Hunters, Allies (language hubs), CPP, Verified.
Events: Weekly Challenge (theme, format, deadline, credit prizes), **Daily Soul Challenge** (generate with Soul / Soul 2.0 / Soul Cinema only, post image + generation link; 3 winners daily, credits), community streams/workshops, feedback sessions on story/visuals/pacing/sound, podcast, monthly awards. Promo codes sometimes given at events.
Useful for learning: study prompts in #prompt-discussion / #learning-hub and Community feed.
