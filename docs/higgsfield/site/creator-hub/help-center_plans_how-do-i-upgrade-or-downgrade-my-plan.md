# How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/plans/how-do-i-upgrade-or-downgrade-my-plan
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (2 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (plans). Upgrade: Pricing -> target plan; immediate, prorated (unused credits and unused annual months are credited), billing date resets. Downgrade: Pricing -> detailed comparison section -> Downgrade; takes effect next cycle, keep current plan until then. Packs/promo credits unaffected.
