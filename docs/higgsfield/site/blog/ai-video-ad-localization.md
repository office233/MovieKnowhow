# How to Localize Video Ads Into Every Language With AI: Dubbing, Lip-Sync, and Reframe

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/blog/ai-video-ad-localization  
Higgsfield, Sep 23, 2026  
Prompts extracted: 0

Localization pipeline from **one approved master ad**.
0. **Master ad in Marketing Studio** (1,500+ templates: product, UGC, fashion, cinematic; product in -> finished ad in one pass). Clear opening + simple product interaction travel best across languages (less pacing drift when audio is replaced).
1. **Translate/adapt the script for timing**, not word-for-word — some languages run longer and break pacing and lip-sync.
2. **Audio -> Translate**: upload video, choose target language -> translated re-voiced track (or upload your own localized VO).
3. **Lipsync Studio** syncs the new audio to the mouth; 7 models: Google Veo 3, Wan 2.5 Speak, Kling Avatars 2.0, Higgsfield Speak 2.0, Infinite Talk, Sync Lipsync 3, Kling Lipsync (img->video and video->video; 18+ languages).
4. Update captions, on-screen text, prices, currencies, dates, CTAs, legal copy manually (not automatic); add subtitles even to dubbed versions (sound-off viewing). Then **Reframe** to 9:16 (TikTok/Reels), 1:1 (feed), 16:9 (YouTube); check safe areas and product visibility after each.

Version matrix: 5 languages x 3 formats = 15 deliverables = 1 master + 4 dubs + 2 extra reframes per language.
Cost for one 15 s localized version (Sep 2026, ~$0.05/credit): Marketing Studio 720p 90 cr ($4.50); Audio translate 45 cr ($2.25); Lipsync Studio with Veo 3 720p 58 cr ($2.90); Reframe 720p 70 cr ($3.50); **total 263 cr ≈ $13.15**.
QA checklist: native-sounding copy reviewed by a native speaker; lip rhythm; pronunciation of names/numbers/brand; regional wording/currency/offers; safe areas; voice-clone permission and actor agreement covering translated/AI versions; likeness rights per market; local ad-claim rules.
