# Higgsfield plugins for Photoshop

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/plugins/photoshop

## Notes

- Native **UXP** plugin for Photoshop 2024+ (macOS Apple Silicon/Intel, Windows). Install: download the `.ccx` (page) / `.uxp` (FAQ) and drop it on the installer; open **Window -> Extensions -> Higgsfield**. Each result arrives as a **new editable layer** (works with masks, smart objects, adjustment layers).
- Headline tools: Generate AI Image; **Realtime AI** (paint or block shapes on the canvas and the AI render updates live with every stroke); **Mockup Studio** (flat design or product shot -> realistic in-context mockup: packaging, devices, cards, scenes); **Layer Decompose** (split a flat image into editable layers); **Angles** (new camera angles of the same subject - the card text mistakenly repeats the Reframe blurb); **Shots** (one frame -> storyboard of wide/close/new perspectives, consistent with the original).
- More tools: Character Swap, Remove Background, Face Swap, Upscale, Skin Enhancer, AI Stylist (outfit try-on). FAQ marketing line: five tools - Angles, Mockup Studio, Expand, Remove BG, Relight.
- FAQ: images processed on Higgsfield's infrastructure and "aren't used for training" (plugin FAQ wording; compare the general Terms of Use in [../legal/terms-privacy-cookies.md](../legal/terms-privacy-cookies.md)); commercial licence on paid plans; vs Adobe Generative Fill - specialised tools (angles, mockups, relight) rather than general fill.
