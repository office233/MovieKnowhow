## Notes

**Topic:** Higgsfield Moodboards = custom trainable style presets for Soul 2.0 images (Feb 24 2026). No prompts.

**Soul 2.0 stack:** Soul ID (character consistency), Soul Reference (reference any image), Soul HEX (colour palette control), built-in presets (Y2K, Digital Camera, Street Photography, Old Smartphone, SWAG Era ...), now **Moodboards** (20+ curated + personal ones).

**Create a personal Moodboard:**
1. Gather up to **80** images sharing one style/palette/theme; ~10 hi-res images is enough, **20-30 is better**.
2. Upload in the Moodboards section (where Soul 2.0 Presets were).
   - No low-quality or blurry images; keep references stylistically cohesive (no mixed styles); **avoid faces** in the batch.
3. Soul 2.0 learns the aesthetic; name the Moodboard.
4. Apply when generating; combinable with **Soul ID** and with an image reference.

**Uses:** consistent social feed aesthetic; turn your photography portfolio's grade into a reusable style; world-building (every character/location in one visual language); brand visuals; music artist covers/promos (pair with Mixed Media for clip visuals).
