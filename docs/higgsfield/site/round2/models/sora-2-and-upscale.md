# Sora 2 landing (/sora2-ai-video) and Sora 2 Upscale

Sources:
- https://higgsfield.ai/sora2-ai-video
- https://higgsfield.ai/sora-2-upscale

Sora 2 is covered in [../../features/models/sora-2.md](../../features/models/sora-2.md) and [../../features/models/sora-2-prompt-guide.md](../../features/models/sora-2-prompt-guide.md); upscaling articles: [../../blog/Upgrade-the-Quality-of-Old-Videos-using-Upscale.md](../../blog/Upgrade-the-Quality-of-Old-Videos-using-Upscale.md).

/sora2-ai-video (canonical /sora-2): 3 steps (reference image -> scenario -> generate); output **480p, 720p or 1080p**; synchronized sound. Otherwise testimonials only.

/sora-2-upscale (canonical /sora-2-enhancer):
- Upload an MP4 (MP4, MOV, AVI, WEBM accepted), choose model and output resolution, download in up to 4K. Web only, typically a few minutes.
- Claimed features: motion fidelity (no artifacts in fast motion), detail recovery, smart denoising, 4K+ scaling with colour balance.
- Upscale models listed: **Sora 2 Enhancer** (by Higgsfield - analyses motion and structure across frames for temporal consistency), **Higgsfield Upscale** (crisp detail and cinematic texture, "optimized for Sora outputs"), **Topaz** (Topaz Labs, clean natural all-rounder).
- Generation tiers promoted beside it: **Sora 2 Max** (versatile, fast) and **Sora 2 Pro Max** (best for complex action and cinematic scenes).
- Workflow: generate at native Sora resolution, then upscale the keeper takes only (cheaper than generating high-res).
