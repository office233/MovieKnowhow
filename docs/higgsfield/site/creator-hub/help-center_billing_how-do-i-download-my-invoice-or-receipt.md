# How do I download my invoice or receipt?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/billing/how-do-i-download-my-invoice-or-receipt
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (2 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (billing). Manage Account -> Subscription -> Upcoming Invoice -> All invoices -> Download (invoices for subscriptions, renewals, packs, Auto-Refill). Company name/address via Billing information -> Manage. Reissue with company details: contact Support with email, invoice date/amount, company data.
