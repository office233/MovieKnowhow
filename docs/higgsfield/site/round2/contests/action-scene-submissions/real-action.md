## Description (author's own words)

> Every hit was real. Every fall was real. For 40 years, one action performer put his body on the line so the action on screen would feel real.

> Now he's 62. His hands shake. It takes ten minutes just to stand up in the morning. And AI can recreate what took him a lifetime to master — in seconds.

> 
> A story about the real people who made AI action possible.
