## Lesson 1 — Intro: One Website, One Cinematic Ad

URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/cinematic-ad-e2e/intro

## Lesson 2 — Stage 1: Building the Assets

URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/cinematic-ad-e2e/stage-1-building-the-assets

- Workspace: in Higgsfield open **Collab** (not Images) - a separate Collab project gives every image and video model in one shared space (good for teams).
- Every asset is made once, locked, reused; take this stage slowly.
- **Product**: one can photo -> **GPT Image 2.0** (strongest editor) -> front/back/top product sheet, so the video model never invents unseen packaging.
- **Character (own likeness)**: **Soul Cinema** with **20 photos** of the creator as character source (builds a reusable avatar). Prompt describes only the layout (full body left, face close-up right) and **skips outfit** so the model proposes looks. Settings: prompt enhancer on, **16:9, 2K, batch of 40**; **8 generations = 1 credit**. Casual outfit chosen (contrasts with the robot suit later).
- **Location** = "the single most important image": video inherits texture and light from the still. Soul Cinema (renders like film stills): empty modern NYC street, sunny, clear sky, anamorphic lens, film grain. 10 variations.
- **Test character + location together** in Seedance (walk down the street, dynamic camera, dutch angle) before building more.
- **Hero robot #7**: Claude writes a structured character-sheet prompt (front/back, white & red armour, number 7) -> Soul Cinema batches with enhancer; reject too-cartoonish / oversized, keep the human-scale one.
- **Rival robot #9**: same prompt style, black & red, number 9 - same world, villain look.
- **Ball**: Claude prompt, chrome/red futuristic panels, locked first pass. **Goal**: futuristic electric energy-shield instead of a net, first generation.

## Lesson 3 — Stage 2: The Prompting Framework

URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/cinematic-ad-e2e/stage-2-the-prompting-framework

- Where most people fail: overloaded prompts, then try -> dislike -> edit -> retry until the idea is a mess.
- Use a Claude Skill packaging Seedance best practices. Install: **Claude Customize -> Skills -> + -> upload**.
- Fresh chat, attach: the **script as PDF** (say explicitly it is the script) + every Stage-1 asset (character, location, both robots, ball, goal).
- Also type each element's **name + short description** (civilian top-up outfit, deserted NYC avenue at midday, full exo-armour robot, opponent robot in black and gunmetal, regulation football, soda can product, electric energy-shield goal) so Claude can reference them precisely.

## Lesson 4 — Scenes 1-2: The Opening & Transformation

URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/cinematic-ad-e2e/scenes-1-2-opening-and-transformation

- **Scene 1 (floating can)** - the most important shot: if the opener feels off, viewers leave. Prompt kept short on purpose (walk, can appears, lands in his hand, handheld with shake); lens, light, acting, physics come from the **style header**. Header also says **no music, environmental sound only** (score added in edit).
- Batch 1 already had real-feeling handheld shake, sun flares, slow-mo drop into the hand. Bonus: the Stage-1 test clip was good enough to reuse as the opening beat; final = best moments of both.
- **Scene 2 (transformation)**: drink, smile, sprint, armour builds mid-air. First 4 batches failed (broken smile, running backwards, blank eyes changing colour, flat transformation).
- Fix by **talking to Claude like a director**: keep moving fast through the transformation, creative handheld angles, run forward, natural smile, alive eyes, no colour change. Second note: add **extreme macro "impact" close-ups** of armour locking (chest plate, wrists, helmet seal).
- Pattern: never hand-edit prompts line by line - watch, describe what's wrong, let Claude rewrite.

## Lesson 5 — Scenes 3-4: First Meeting & The Goal

URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/cinematic-ad-e2e/scenes-3-4-first-meeting-and-the-goal

- **Scene 3 (rival arrives with the ball)**: two passes were static wides with barely moving robots.
- Fix = different kind of prompt: tell Claude **exactly what the camera sees, shot by shot** (close-up on the rival's leg sprinting with ball -> over-the-shoulder from behind the hero -> hero drops into a ready stance), with camera direction per shot: **handheld, shaky, low-angle, cinematic**. Low shaky on-field camera = real sports-ad tension.
- Keep good unused generations - they can resurface in later scenes.
- **Scene 4 (goal forms)**: three short punchy close-ups (electric blue light cracking through asphalt, goal building from the lines, goal exploding as the ball passes). Short high-impact inserts make the edit feel expensive; easiest scene. Explosion didn't land; the rest was used.
