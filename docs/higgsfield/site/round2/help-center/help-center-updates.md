## Plans (updated Sep 14, 2026)
- Two families: **individual** plans and **business** plans (**Team, Scale, Enterprise**). Prices only on /pricing (interactive plan picker + comparison table).
- Individual plans differ by: model access (entry = core models; higher = every model incl. new releases, which may roll out gradually), monthly credits (reset at renewal, no rollover), **parallel generations**, and **Unlimited** access to selected models on mid/top plans (a one-time purchase, not recurring; which models and for how long depends on plan).
- Monthly or annual billing (annual cheaper per month). One active subscription per account. **No free trial**; the free tier has a small model set and no credits.
- Business: credits pooled across seats. **Scale** adds more credits per seat, priority queue, SSO, admin spend controls. **Enterprise**: custom volumes, **rollover credits**, dedicated capacity, compliance docs, user groups with limits.

## Upgrade / downgrade (Sep 14, 2026)
- Upgrade: immediate; billing date resets; you pay the new price minus the prorated unused value of the current plan (unused credits and unused months count). Promo and Credit Pack credits untouched.
- Downgrade: via the **comparison section below the plan cards** on /pricing; takes effect next billing cycle; credits reset to the new allowance then.
- Upgrade refund: within 7 days if no credits from the new plan were used - refunds only the upgrade difference. Credit Packs keep their **90-day** validity and Auto-Refill settings carry over. Annual -> monthly switch is not possible mid-term.

## Refunds for annual plans (Aug 31, 2026)
- All-or-nothing: full refund only within **7 days** of purchase and with **no credits used** (or as local law requires), minus a service fee of **up to 6%**; the subscription ends immediately. No prorated refunds afterwards: keep using the plan, turn off auto-renew, or downgrade at renewal.

## Promo codes (Sep 22, 2026)
- Manage Account -> Promocode -> Enter code; applied instantly. Promo credits expire on the date shown and survive cancellation (usable on free-plan models until expiry). One activation per code; Support-issued codes expire if not activated within 7 days. Sources: official socials first, Discord events, and case-by-case Support goodwill.

## Account deletion (Sep 12, 2026)
- Manage Account -> Personal Profile -> Manage Account Deletion -> confirm checklist -> Delete. Subscription cancels automatically. **30-day** restore window (sign back in); afterwards generations, Soul characters, presets, credits are gone. Content already used for model training cannot be removed from trained models. Can't log in? Email support@higgsfield.ai.

## DMCA, IP, NCII, commercial use (Sep 14, 2026)
- DMCA and IP notices to support@higgsfield.ai (six required elements; acknowledgement within 48 h; counter-notification possible). GDPR/CCPA to privacy@higgsfield.ai.
- Wrongly blocked by the IP filter? Email support with approximate time, model and prompt (blocked generations have no ID).
- Non-consensual intimate imagery removed within **48 hours** of a valid report.
- **Commercial use**: Terms section 4.4 - Higgsfield claims no ownership of inputs/outputs and does not restrict commercial use for any user; no separate licence to buy. **Indemnification against third-party IP claims only on Enterprise.** Tax and security documents (W-9, SOC 2, DPA...) only for Enterprise.
