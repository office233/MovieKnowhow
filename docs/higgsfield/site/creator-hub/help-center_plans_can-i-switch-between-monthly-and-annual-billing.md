# Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/plans/can-i-switch-between-monthly-and-annual-billing
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (2 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (plans). Monthly -> annual anytime (Pricing -> toggle Annual -> Change Commitment): immediate, prorated, billing date resets, subscription credits reset to annual allowance then every 30 days. Annual -> monthly not possible; cancel auto-renewal and subscribe monthly after the year ends. Refunds all-or-nothing within the refund window.
