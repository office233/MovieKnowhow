## Notes

**Topic:** two skills in Higgsfield MCP (Claude/ChatGPT) and Supercomputer (Aug 17 2026).

**Faceless Content Factory:** topic -> animated YouTube video **up to 10 min** with script, narration, animation, music, subtitles. **13 styles** incl. Stickman Cartoon (simple/kid-friendly), Editorial Motion Graphics (documentary/news authority), Watercolor Chronicle (soft historical), Fairy Tale & Myth, Claymotion (tactile/playful), Paper Diorama, Whiteboard Doodle, Pixel Art. One sentence is enough; or write a detailed brief with beats, pacing, tone (example below: 58 s, 16:9, STYLE block, no on-screen text).
**UGC Factory:** product idea + product photo (+ optional creator reference) -> concept, script, vertical video with the **same avatar throughout**. 6 templates: Unboxing (launch reveal), Product Review, SaaS, Try-On (wearables/clothes), Tutorial, Localization (same voice/pacing in another language). Finished videos exceed the 15 s single-generation limit because the skill assembles multiple segments.

**Setup:** Claude: Customize -> Connectors -> `https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp`. ChatGPT: Plugins Directory -> Higgsfield -> Add -> sign in. Supercomputer needs no MCP setup.

**Workflows:** A) Faceless via MCP: connect -> pick style + describe topic -> watch stages (video, narration, montage, subtitles) -> review one combined file. B) UGC via Supercomputer: attach creator photo + product photo -> one-sentence ad description (skeptical-to-sold arc, product action on camera, closing line) -> review.

**Real costs:** Faceless 1 min 1080p = style key + 3-4 asset images 12 cr + 6 video blocks x 30 = 180 cr + narration (11 takes incl. 5 rewrites) 12 cr + 1080p upscale 17.5 cr = **221.5 cr ($11.08)**.
