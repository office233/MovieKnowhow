# My generation is stuck or failed. What should I do?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/troubleshooting/generation-is-stuck-or-failed
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (3 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (troubleshooting). Status meanings: Queued (hover tile -> Cancel -> retry; instant refund), Processing (wait), Generating (no cancel). Errors: "Something went wrong" (temporary or captcha skipped -> refresh, complete captcha), "limit of generations at the same time" (cancel a queued job or wait), "Rejected due to copyright restrictions"/"IP detected", NSFW flag, "Failed, Credits refunded". Credit gens use the priority queue; Unlimited/free gens a shared pool (slower). Failed gens auto-refund within minutes except **Grok (charged when generation starts)**. Very long/complex prompts or heavy inputs can fail: simplify.
