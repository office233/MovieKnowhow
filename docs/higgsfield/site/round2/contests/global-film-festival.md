## Rules in brief (paraphrased)

**Who and how many**
- Any registered Higgsfield user aged 18+; no paid plan needed to enter or win. Solo or teams of up to 4; the Team Leader owns the Cinema Studio festival project and receives any prize.
- Unlimited entries, each a separate film; near-duplicates may be merged. **One prize per film and per person/team.**
- Excluded: Higgsfield staff, jurors, screeners and their households. Creative partners not involved in judging may enter.

**The film**
- **Minimum 3:00** (hard rule); up to 5:00 recommended, longer is still eligible. MP4 or MOV up to 4K. Any aspect ratio (16:9 or 21:9 recommended). Any language, but English subtitles or voice-over are required.
- Official Higgsfield watermark and packshot (available inside your submission project) must be on the final video and on the social version.
- **All AI image and video generation must happen inside the dedicated Cinema Studio festival project on higgsfield.ai** - any model on the platform is fine; generating AI imagery anywhere else is disqualifying.
- Traditional tools (Blender, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere, Photoshop...) are allowed for editing, compositing, grading, titles and non-AI 3D, as long as they are not used to create new AI imagery.
- **All audio must be AI-generated** (music, voice, lipsync, dialogue, SFX). Human-recorded or licensed music is banned even if you own it. AI audio may predate the window, but every file must be uploaded into the project and its tool identifiable.
- Official Higgsfield open-source packs (Hell Grind etc.) and Academy drops may be used freely.
- **After the deadline every eligible project becomes public**: film, prompts, generation history and assets. Footage that does not match the project's generation history can be disqualified - keep every asset inside the project.
- Minimum viable submission: final video + a public social post (Instagram, YouTube, X or Reddit) from a public account, left up until the festival ends. The post is a requirement, not a scoring factor; a private account is the most common reason for rejection.

**Banned content**: third-party IP, sexual content, violence against real people, politics, religion, illegal content, and **any identifiable real person's face or voice - including your own, with or without consent**. Fictional child characters are allowed only in non-sexual, non-violent, non-harmful contexts.

## Judging

1. Screening for eligibility (runtime, format, content, generation history, submission completeness).
2. Higgsfield's creative team builds a shortlist using the four criteria: **Directing & Storytelling; Visual Craft; Sound & Editing; Originality & Concept**. Social numbers are not used.
3. In parallel on the same shortlist: community vote (only users with an active **paid** plan can vote) decides Audience Choice; an independent jury picks 1st-3rd and the honorable mentions. The jury announced in the blog post: Edwin Catmull, Phedon Papamichael, Paul W.S. Anderson.

## Other obligations worth knowing before you enter

- Higgsfield may audit sources (generation history, prompts, project files, audio tool info) at any time; keep them for **24 months**.
- You keep copyright, but grant Higgsfield a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to use the film, clips, stills and generation history for festival promotion and product marketing, including paid ads.
- Higgsfield does not mediate copying disputes between participants; the contest is governed by California law and the Terms of Use arbitration clause; liability capped at $10,000.

## Practical checklist derived from the rules

1. Create the Cinema Studio festival project first and generate every shot inside it.
2. Cast only fictional characters (Soul ID / Soul Cast) - no face or voice references of real people, not even yourself.
3. Produce all music, dialogue and SFX with AI tools and upload each file into the project; log which tool made it.
4. Edit and grade wherever you like; do not use outside generative tools for imagery.
5. Export at least 3:00 with English subtitles or VO, watermark and packshot included.
6. Post publicly from a public account and keep it up until results are announced.
