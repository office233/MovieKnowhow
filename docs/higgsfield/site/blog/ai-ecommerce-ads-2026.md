# How to Make AI Video Ads and Posters for Ecommerce in 2026

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/blog/ai-ecommerce-ads-2026  
Higgsfield, Aug 19, 2026  
Prompts extracted: 6

Three e-commerce formats: **product hero shot** (catalog/listing image), **marketplace banner** (listing-page layout: headline, price/spec callout, product placement), **motion** (camera movement on product; also motion graphics for SaaS/app from logo/website). Inputs: text-to-video (concepts), image-to-video (existing product photo — preferred), URL-to-video (live page).
Two workflows:
- **A. Marketing Studio template** (low control, fast, no prompt): 6 categories (product shots, ads, marketplace banners, posters, UGC, motion) -> template -> paste product URL (imagery, logo, colors, copy) -> generate -> review product accuracy, text, branding, hook, placement.
- **B. Seedance 2.5 direct control** (high control): up to **30 s per clip**, **up to 50 references** to lock shape/color/packaging, **era selector** 1960s–2020s via a prompt line ("change the decade to…"), detailed prompt.
Costs: hero shot or banner 2K = 8 credits ($0.40); Marketing Studio motion 15 s 720p = 89 cr ($4.45); Seedance 2.5 15 s 720p = 98 cr ($4.90); 30 s 720p = 195 cr ($9.75).
Weekly planning: new theme/style/format every day (hero -> banner -> motion…), make each day look distinct; build it in **Canvas** as a reusable template, duplicate weekly (credits only charged when nodes generate).
Hooks decide the first 2 seconds: curiosity, urgency, or an unspoken feeling.
**Mega-prompt anatomy (Seedance 2.5 UGC)**: reference key in attach order (product / character "identity only, NOT lighting" / location); hands & phone rules (phone is the camera, never visible); duration; HARD RULES for natural motion, living realism (blink every 2–4 s, micro-expressions), product physics (gum tape stays firm, never slime), no glow; **"relight the subject to match the location"** (discard studio light from the character ref); style (authentic smartphone UGC, 9:16, eye level); two shots with one hard cut hidden inside a motion blur; timecoded actions and dialogue; AVOID list.
