# How One of Korea's Leading Digital Agencies, AdQUA, Cut AI Production Costs by 97% with Higgsfield

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/case-studies/adqua-case-study
Published: Jul 31, 2026 (8 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: customer story (Korea; ad agency AdQUA Interactive + sister brand-builder Boosterz, parent FSN). Tools: Cinema Studio, Soul ID.

**Numbers**
- Boosterz, LINGTEA "Night tea" promo: ~97% lower production cost, ~79% less time, ~85% fewer people vs. traditional shoot-and-edit.
- AdQUA: production time ~1/10, operating cost ~1/50 of before.
- AdQUA YouTube channel "Movie Trailer Only" (영화예고만): Korean pro-baseball stories retold as movie trailers starring custom mascot characters; 15M cumulative views, 120k watch hours since launch.
- Two new beauty brands (GIPPEUN launch, LuluPeach performance campaigns) produced 100% on Higgsfield: key visuals, ad creative, SNS content.

**Workflow / techniques**
1. Ideation + prompt design in an LLM (they name Claude), then generate images/video on Higgsfield, then finish in an editor. Planners and marketers (not just creatives) prototype directly.
2. Character-series method: design a polished original character with an image model -> lock it as a fixed reference ("character lock" / Soul ID) -> generate many videos by giving that same character new actions and environments. This is what made a recurring mascot channel viable (previous tools needed endless regeneration to keep the character).
3. Genre-parody formats that worked on YouTube: fan parody, mascot thriller parody, cinematic animal-character spot, folk-tale action parody, K-drama episode, mascot press conference.
4. Fast camera work + action sequences for sports-trailer style were handled with Cinema Studio camera control.

**Lessons**
- Consolidating many subscriptions into one platform removes tool-evaluation overhead and inconsistent team workflows.
- Scaling to more brands no longer scales production headcount proportionally.
