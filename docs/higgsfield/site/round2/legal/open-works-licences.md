# Open Works licences (OWL) and the Originals study-only licence (HOL-RO)

Sources:
- https://higgsfield.ai/licences/owl-p-1.0
- https://higgsfield.ai/licences/owl-p-nl-1.0
- https://higgsfield.ai/licences/owl-nc-1.0
- https://higgsfield.ai/licences/hol-ro-1.0

Higgsfield publishes film releases **together with their production build** (prompts, prompt chains, negative prompts, parameters, seeds, model/preset choices, workflows/node graphs, character sheets, references, intermediate and final generations). What you may do with that build depends on the licence badge on the release. Which Originals use which licence: see [../../films-site/series/](../../films-site/series/) (per those notes: Kok Boru = OWL-P-1.0; Hell Grind, Oneiric, Adiliada = OWL-P-NL-1.0; Zephyr and Zephyr Special = OWL-NC-1.0; The Cully Hill Boys = HOL-RO-1.0).

| Licence | Short meaning | Commercial use | Derivatives / "universe development" | Reuse the prompts & assets to generate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **OWL-P-1.0** Permissive | "the MIT of the family" | yes | yes (sequels, fan films, remixes) | yes, **only on the Higgsfield platform** |
| **OWL-P-NL-1.0** Permissive, No Likeness | as OWL-P, but assets in the Schedule of Excluded Assets and the **character likeness** they embody stay closed | yes | yes - you may continue the story and name the character, but must cast a visually different actor | yes except excluded assets |
| **OWL-NC-1.0** Non-Commercial Universe | build on the world, fan works, teaching | **no** (no ads, sponsored, client or paid work, merch) | yes, non-commercial; same terms must pass downstream | yes, on platform, non-commercial |
| **HOL-RO-1.0** Higgsfield Originals, Study Only (The Cully Hill Boys) | read-only: watch and inspect the build on the platform | no | **no** derivative works of any kind | **no** - not as prompt, seed, parameter, workflow or reference, on any system; no downloading or dataset use |

Common OWL conditions (all three OWL variants):
- **All generation from the materials must happen on Higgsfield**; the materials must not be fed into any other generative tool. Audio, music, and anything the platform does not offer (3D, motion graphics, titles, captions) may be made elsewhere, as long as the materials are not used as inputs there.
- Normal post-production outside the platform is fine (editing, grading, retouching for continuity, stabilising, denoising, upscaling, interpolation, compositing, mastering) - but not creating new characters or imagery outside.
- Credit line in the form "<Work title> by <Creator>, licensed under OWL-P-1.0", link to the licence, say if you modified the materials, keep notices and watermarks/C2PA credentials.
- No repackaging the materials as asset packs, prompt packs, datasets, models or presets.
- **Showcase licence**: publishing something made from the materials lets the creator and Higgsfield feature it (incl. paid promotion of the platform); you can ask them to stop new uses (within 30 days).
- No rights clearance is warranted: brands, products, artworks or real people may appear in the materials - clearing them is your job, stricter for commercial use. Liability capped at USD 100; the licence is granted by the creator, Higgsfield is not the licensor (except HOL-RO, where Higgsfield is licensor).
- Breach terminates automatically; first curable breach reinstated if fixed within 30 days.

HOL-RO specifics: human study only (no automated processing); quotes limited to **30 seconds of footage or 3 still frames** per treatment, credited "THE CULLY HILL BOYS (2026), a Higgsfield Original. Licensed for study under HOL-RO-1.0. Not licensed for reuse."; excerpts of the build must not allow a prompt chain or workflow to be reconstructed. This knowledge base therefore does not reproduce Cully Hill Boys prompts.

How to apply an OWL licence to your own release: publish a Notice (Open Work title and year, creator, licence id, materials released, rights notes, link to the licence text) and include it as a LICENSE file in the release package.
