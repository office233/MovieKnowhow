## Notes

**Topic:** Shorts Studio — styling existing footage into Shorts (Jul 2 2026). No prompts.
- Input: your own video up to **2 min** (not text-to-video); 40+ style presets (Bold Urban, Green Contrast, Urban Serenity, Warm Glow...) that add text treatment + graphic overlays; or **Create a Preset from References** (custom brand look). Output 9:16 or 16:9 at **720p**, ~**$4 per 25 s clip**. Generations queue in background.
- Steps: trim to the best segment (the first 2 s must be action, not establishing context) -> higgsfield.ai/shorts-studio -> pick preset -> generate -> watch whole clip (hook in first 2 s, pacing/graphics issues in the middle third) -> export MP4 -> upload (check Shorts length rules).
- Performance rules: hook in frame one; one clear point per Short; design for vertical; captions (most watch muted; for synced captions on speech route through LipSync Studio).
- Volume: batch a series with **one preset** (same preset = a produced show; five presets = five unrelated experiments); custom preset for strict brand identity; generate footage with Kling 3.0/Veo 3.1 + Soul ID first, then style in Shorts Studio.
- Alternatives: InVideo (prompt/footage/templates), HeyGen (avatar talking heads, auto localisation), Canva (templates).
