# How Emmy Winning Kerri Zane Optimizes Production Pipeline with Cinema Studio

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/case-studies/kerri-zane-emmy-producer-ai-film-test-higgsfield
Published: Sep 10, 2026 (5 mins read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: customer story (Kerri Zane Enterprises; Emmy-winning TV producer, now microdramas and brand storytelling). Tool: Cinema Studio.

**Numbers**: 25-40% less time on concepting/pre-production (1-2 working days saved on a short film/campaign concept); 30-50% lower previs/early visualization cost; moving concepts within hours, client-ready direction same day, substantial first pass of a campaign/film sequence in 24-72 h; a feature-film test (put an actress from real performance footage into a time-travel scenario without building period sets) finished in under 8 hours against a 24-hour brief; 2-3 projects in parallel.

**Techniques**
- Show clients motion from the first meeting, not stills: approval of a static board often flips once the idea moves. Kill weak directions early.
- Workflow: approved hero frame -> image-to-video in Cinema Studio.
- Use camera movement presets to test the same shot as slow push-in, tracking move, handheld, or aggressive move without rebuilding the concept ("think like a director instead of hoping the model interprets the prompt").
- Frame-based control: anchor start and end frames of a shot.
- Build several visual treatments of the same idea in parallel and present real choices within one working day.
- Higgsfield sits inside the existing stack (other tools still used for images, edit, sound, finishing).
