## Notes

**Launch post (Mar 30 2026).** What 3.0 added:
- Physics-aware generation (collisions with weight, tire friction in drifts, debris with gravity) — for fights, chases, explosions.
- Character identity locked across frames (face, body proportions, clothing).
- Cleaner complex motion (walk/run/fight/dance/turn without jitter).
- **Cinematic reasoning engine**: feed **up to 9 reference images** (characters + locations created in Cinema Studio 3.0), describe the story; no need to set start/end frames per shot.
- **Native audio**: SFX, speech and music generated in sync.
- **Genres**: General, Action, Horror, Comedy, Noir, Drama, Epic — genre changes pacing, motion energy, lighting and camera logic, not just a filter.
- **8 speed-ramp presets**: Linear, Auto, Flash In, Flash Out, Slow-mo, Bullet Time, Impact, Ramp Up.

**How to use:** (1) upload up to 9 refs (characters+locations) -> (2) prompt the story/narrative intent, not per-frame details -> (3) pick genre, speed ramp, camera movement; rig (sensor, lens, focal length, aperture) optional -> (4) generate, review, iterate.

**Use-case tips:** action = physics + Bullet Time/Impact ramps; music videos = Noir/Drama/Epic genres + ramps, mute native audio if scoring yourself; brand spots ship with SFX/VO/music baked in; episodic series = reuse cast + locations for continuity.
