# How do I get a refund for an accidental charge?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/refunds/how-do-i-get-a-refund-for-an-accidental-charge
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (3 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (refunds). Accidental upgrade, annual-instead-of-monthly, accidental pack, duplicate charge: standard refund process, contact Support ASAP (7-day window) with account email, date, amount, description. Check All invoices first (bank authorization holds drop off). Renewals (even unnoticed) are not refundable.
