### P1. NYC SoHo crosswalk — spaceship overhead and police chase (five-shot sequence)
- Use-case: Cinematic film scene | Model: Cinema Studio 4.0 (i2v, Soul ID character + location reference) | Settings: 15 s continuous sequence, 16:9, 5 cuts, 35mm/50mm

```text
Cinematic 15-second continuous sequence, 16:9, i2v. Character: the young man from the character reference, beige brushed-wool zip bomber jacket, grey turtleneck under white tee, dark indigo wide-leg jeans, brown loafers, slicked-back blond hair. Location: the New York SoHo street from the location reference, cast-iron facades, fire escapes, parked yellow cabs, hazy backlit sky down the avenue. 0-3s: Wide static shot, low tripod height, 35mm. He steps onto the zebra crosswalk and takes exactly two slow steps, then stops mid-stride as a deep mechanical hum builds overhead, an unfamiliar low-frequency drone layered over distant police sirens. He freezes, listening, weight still on the front foot. 3-6s: Cut to medium shot, chest-up, 50mm, shallow depth of field, street softly blurred behind him. Still standing in place, he turns his head sharply left, then right, then tilts it slightly as the humming grows, searching for the source of the sound. Jaw tightening, eyes narrowing. Sirens rise and echo off the facades. Micro handheld drift. 6-10s: Cut to extreme low angle from ground level, camera tilting up past his shoulder. A colossal dark metallic spaceship glides slowly along the length of the street directly overhead, moving away from camera deeper down the avenue, filling the frame, panel-lined underbelly with pulsing blue running lights, casting a huge moving shadow that sweeps over him and the parked cars. He tilts his head back and stares up, lips parting in awe. Deep sub-bass rumble overwhelming the sirens, dust and paper debris lifting off the asphalt, volumetric light rays breaking around the hull. 10-13s: Cut to over-the-shoulder shot from directly behind him, his back and beige jacket filling the left third of frame, slightly out of focus. Camera looks down the avenue in the same direction the spaceship is heading. Three police cars tear past him one after another, driving away from camera down the street, chasing after the ship in the same direction it flies, roof lightbars strobing red and blue across the asphalt and his jacket, Doppler sirens ripping past. He stays planted, head turning to follow them. Gentle forward gimbal push, motion blur on the vehicles, red-blue taillight glow receding into the haze. 13-15s: Cut to final wide static shot. He stands alone and small in the middle of the empty crosswalk, back to camera, watching the spaceship and the police lights shrink together into the hazy skyline at the end of the street. Steam drifting from a manhole, distant fading sirens, low ominous drone. Slow subtle push-in. Backlit natural daylight, teal-and-amber cinematic grade, atmospheric haze, film grain, 35mm/50mm lenses, shallow depth of field, consistent character wardrobe and face throughout. No text, no captions, no watermarks.
```
