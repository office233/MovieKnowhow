**Outpainting (2 credits).** Upload an image, pick a target aspect ratio (the UI default shows 21:9; the FAQ lists 16:9, 9:16, 4:3, 1:1), and generate. New content matches the original's lighting, perspective and texture, and the original pixels are left untouched. No prompt. **Technique:** reformat one hero frame for YouTube (16:9), Stories (9:16) and feed (1:1). You can also widen a portrait keyframe to 21:9 before animating for a cinematic ratio.
