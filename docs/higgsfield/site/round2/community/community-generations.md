What the feed shows about how people actually prompt (lessons):
- Long **structured prompts** dominate the most-liked posts: JSON-style blocks (`character_identity`, `meta: aspect_ratio / quality / resolution`) and labelled sections ("CRITICAL REALISM", "IMAGE 1 is the absolute and only master reference"). They are used mainly to **lock identity from an uploaded photo** and to force raw, un-retouched phone realism.
- **Reference-edit prompts** follow a fixed pattern: declare which image is the master reference, list exactly what must stay identical (composition, number of people, product details), then the single change.
- Soul 2.0 prompts are written as photographic descriptions (framing, subject, wardrobe, light, camera/phone look) - consistent with the Soul guidance elsewhere in this knowledge base.
- Many short one-liners (upscale, resize, "front view") exist too; they are listed below and not treated as prompts.
