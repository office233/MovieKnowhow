**Urban Cuts** (also called "BeatFit" in text) — one photo -> beat-synced outfit video.
Flow: upload photo (face fully visible, clearly lit) -> upload your outfit photo or pick from **40+ outfit presets** (streetwear, clean-fit, Y2K, techwear, high fashion, athleisure, seasonal, editorial, quiet luxury, Korean clean-fit, retro sportswear…) -> upload audio or pick from **30+ preset audios** (trending, fast beats, trap loops, summer ambience) -> generate. It analyses the waveform peaks and places cuts/transitions on the beats.
Formats: 9:16, 1:1, 3:4, 4:3, 16:9.
Why it works: music-synced cuts boost retention on TikTok/Reels/Shorts; lets creators respond instantly to new trending sounds; OOTD content from a single mirror selfie; lookbook/drop reels for fashion brands without models or filming; 2–5 videos/day volume.
