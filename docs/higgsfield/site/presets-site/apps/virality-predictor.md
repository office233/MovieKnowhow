## What it does

Predict how viral your video hook is before you post

**How it works (from page):** upload a clip up to **15 s** (vertical or horizontal): a hook under test, a pre-launch ad, or a draft reel. A modeled audience "watches" it and the tool maps predicted brain response across vision, sound, memory, attention and language. Output: a **virality/engagement score** (one number for predicted watch time), a **peak hook timestamp**, a **hold rate**, and a **brain-region heatmap** showing which regions the clip activates.

**How to use it in a pipeline:** treat it as a test screening for hooks. Generate 2-4 alternative openings (e.g. with the MCP Hook Multiplier skill), score each here, and publish the one with the best hold rate and earliest peak. Keep test clips at 15 s or less.
