## What it does

Take full control of your image's atmosphere and lighting. Manually adjust light direction, color temperature, and shadows on any photo with our AI.

**AI relighting (custom tool, Pro).** Manually set light **direction/position, intensity, color temperature and brightness**, and control how light wraps the subject. Presets exist but you are not limited to them. Use it to fix under-exposed photos (add virtual sources, lift shadows) or to relight a keyframe so it matches the lighting of the next shot before animating.
