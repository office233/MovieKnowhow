# How to Create 100+ Ad Variations With AI: Swap Hooks, Products, and Languages

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/blog/100-plus-ad-variations-ai  
Higgsfield, Sep 18, 2026  
Prompts extracted: 0

Method: one master ad, then **change one layer at a time**. 5 hooks x 5 products x 4 languages (original + 3 dubs) = 100 ads (5 total languages would be 125).
Variation axes: opening hook; creator/character; gesture/movement; background/location; lighting; seasonal visuals; offer/CTA; language/VO; platform format.

Tools per step:
1. **Marketing Studio** master ad (1,500+ templates; pick template, add product, one-click result). Choose a master with clear opening, simple product interaction, composition that tolerates similar-sized products (very different dimensions/hand positions break swaps).
2. **Genjutsu Object Swap** — replace product from a reference image; keeps camera, motion, composition, lighting, hand interaction. For models/colors/flavors/same product line.
3. **Genjutsu Motion Transfer** — reuse the strongest hook performance (motion, gestures, tempo, camera) with a new creator/scene.
4. **Seedance 2.5 Edit** — clip ≤30 s + up to 50 reference photos/videos; prompt or **Draw to Edit** a region; change background (snow -> beach), outfit, props, atmosphere while action stays.
5. **Relight** (video) — presets Silhouette, Practicals, Window, Overhead Fall, Contre Jour, Soft Cross; Auto mode; text prompt; manual control of up to 2 lights (position, color, brightness, diffusion).
6. **Audio** translate/re-voice -> review -> **Lipsync Studio** (18 languages, 7 models: Google Veo 3, Wan 2.5 Speak, Kling Avatars 2.0, Higgsfield Speak 2.0, Infinite Talk, Sync Lipsync 3, Kling Lipsync; Veo 3 & Wan 2.5 Speak have 15+ presets).
7. **Reframe** to 9:16/1:1/16:9; check captions/product/safe areas.
Scale note: Genjutsu, Seedance 2.5 Edit and Lipsync Studio are one result at a time (no batch button) — use **Supercomputer or Canvas** for parallel jobs/reusable pipelines.
Costs (10 s): Marketing Studio 720p 60 cr ($3.00); Genjutsu Object Swap/Motion Transfer 720p 71.5 cr ($3.58); Seedance 2.5 Edit 720p 66 cr ($3.30); Relight 90 cr ($4.50); Lipsync (Veo 3) 720p 58 cr ($2.90). Cost scales per layer changed.
QA: product recognizable, legible logos/packaging, natural hand interaction, smooth motion, lighting match, natural translation, lip match, market-correct offer/currency/CTA, native review, face/voice permission, legal claims, safe areas.
