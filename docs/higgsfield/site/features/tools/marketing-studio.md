# Marketing Studio (URL-to-ad, AI avatars, ad modes)

Sources: https://higgsfield.ai/marketing-studio-intro, /marketing-automation, /ai-ad-generator, /ai-avatar-generator ("powered by Hermes Agent").

## Core workflow
1. **Add product**: paste a product or website URL (name, description, images auto-extracted) or upload up to 5 images; products are saved and reusable. An **app URL** produces screen-demo UGC instead of a product ad.
2. **Pick an avatar**: 40+ library presenters (other copy: 100+) or describe one and Soul 2.0 generates it; or upload your own photo to make an avatar. Pin/rename/reuse to keep one brand face.
3. **Choose a mode** (below).
4. **Generate** - script, shots, camera and edit are automatic; batch variations across formats/avatars/channels for A/B tests.

Engines: video = **Seedance 2.0** (native audio, lip-sync, physics-aware motion); images = Nano Banana; avatars = Soul 2.0. Aspect 9:16, 16:9, 1:1; duration configurable. Default output language English unless specified; avatars can speak other languages. Avatars can hold/wear/apply/demonstrate products with correct mechanics. Royalty-free, commercial use.

## Ad modes (what each produces)
| Mode | Look |
|---|---|
| TV Spot | broadcast-style ad, cinematic camera, real locations, story arc |

| Unboxing | package arrives, tape rips, reveal, genuine reaction |
| UGC Virtual Try On | person films themselves wearing the product at home |
| Hyper Motion | pure CGI product hero, dynamic camera, VFX, no people/VO |
| Pro Virtual Try On | product in motion on city streets, tracking shots, editorial posing |
| Wild Card | AI invents characters, locations, VFX, story around the product |
