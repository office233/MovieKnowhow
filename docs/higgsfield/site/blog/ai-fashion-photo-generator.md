**Soul 2.0** (Higgsfield's own fashion-aware foundation image model) + four "brand lock" anchors:
1. Style: **Moodboard** — 20+ photos in one cohesive style, **no faces**.
2. Color: **Soul HEX** — extract palette from a reference image (built-in palettes: Film Colors, Lime Jam, Candy Pink, Nostalgic Blue, Soft Palette, Black Gloss); **Color Transfer** applies a finished grade to other outputs.
3. Identity: **Soul ID** — 20+ photos of the person from multiple angles; same face in images and videos.
4. Look: **one preset per series**. Presets: Warm Ambient, Retro BW, Y2K Street, Subtle Flash, Y2K Studio, Street Photography, Theatrical Light, Asian Nostalgia, Editorial Street Style, Surreal Solarization, Flash Editorial, Digital Camera, Siren, Swag Era, Mystique City, Balletcore, Candy Pop, 2000s Band, Frutiger Aero, Drain, Old Smartphone.
Specs: quality 1.5k (fast/cheap) or 2k (best fidelity); ratios 9:16, 3:4, 2:3, 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 3:2; built-in prompt enhance.

Why AI fashion looks flat: leaving everything to the prompt. **Prompt** = direction (aesthetic/era/mood, simple clothing, location type, pose). **References** = what must not drift (face/skin tone, light temperature/color, fabric texture, background details, palette).

Campaign workflow:
1. Set the four anchors.
2. Hero shot at **2k**, aesthetic direction first in the prompt, **emotion cues in brackets**, ratio 3:4 or 2:3 editorial / 9:16 social.
3. Approve hero (face, color, aesthetic hold).
4. Multiply at **1.5k**, 4 at a time (1/4 control), **change one element per pass** (lighting -> composition -> styling); re-render winners at 2k.
5. Color Transfer the hero grade across the set.
6. QC all images.
Extras: **AI Stylist** outfit swap ≈ $0.10/generation (10 looks for $1); **Urban Cuts** beat-synced outfit video from a full-length photo, $1.70 per generation (music moods e.g. "Tokyo Dust", "Vapor Skin").
