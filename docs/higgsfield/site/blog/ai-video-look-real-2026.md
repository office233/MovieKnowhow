# How to Make AI Video Look Real in 2026: Lighting, Motion and Camera Control

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/blog/ai-video-look-real-2026  
Higgsfield, Aug 17, 2026  
Prompts extracted: 6

Three tells of fake AI video: **light without a named source** (flat, even), **motion without weight** (floaty walk cycles), **a camera that is too stable/smooth**. "Cinematic", "realistic", "high quality" are not instructions — describe physics instead.
Fixes:
- **Light**: name the actual source (window, lamp, torch), its direction and falloff; declare it the *only* source; define how the face lighting changes when the source moves.
- **Motion**: describe what the weight does (foot to foot, limp, hesitation), object mass (cup with wrist corrections), secondary motion (hair, cloth).
- **Camera**: give it a reason to be imperfect — breathing sway, footstep pulse, operator walking backward at subject's pace, small human reframes, "never gimbal-smooth"; shallow DoF helps only combined with named light + weighted motion.
**Strong-prompt template (used in all three examples)**: SCENE CONTEXT -> CHARACTER -> LOCATION MAP -> FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING (exact x/y % positions; "first visible frame already contains the full staging — no empty establishing frame") -> FORMAT MODE (single continuous take, real-time) -> OPTICS (diagonal FOV in degrees, distance) -> CAMERA -> ACTION TIMING (timecoded) -> PHYSICS -> LIGHTING (hierarchy of sources) -> AUDIO -> POSITIVE LOCKS (identity lock, steam only from the cup, never looks at camera, film stock e.g. Kodak Vision3 250D/500T, "Lubezki-style natural-light realism").
Tools: **Cinema Studio 4.0** (6 lighting presets, 30+ camera movement presets, 3 apertures, genre-driven physics) so the prompt only describes the action; **Seedance 2.5** (lighting-angle selector, camera/lens settings, **physics selector: realistic, superhero, hyperbolic**, up to 50 refs); **Kling 3.0** (per-segment shot size/perspective/move, up to 6 cuts, cloth/hair/fluid/collision sim); **WAN 2.7** (motion, camera path and physics carried from a reference clip; phoneme-level lip sync).
Cost per 15 s 720p: Cinema Studio 4.0 98 cr ($4.90); Seedance 2.5 98 cr ($4.90); Kling 3.0 30 cr ($1.50); WAN 2.7 23 cr ($1.15).
Final check: obvious light source; footfalls; camera not too steady; settings still work on the next shot; convincing at a glance.

### P4. STRONG motion/light prompt — torch-lit forest walk
- Use-case: Cinematic film scene | Model: Seedance 2.5 / Kling 3.0 / Cinema Studio | Settings: 10 s single take, 84° wide, handheld retreat, torch-only key

```text
A lone man walks straight toward the camera through a dark twilight forest, sweeping a burning torch in his right hand as he inspects the dark ahead. Every move of the torch visibly changes the light on his face, while a faint cold blue-hour glow shapes the forest behind him. The shot is a study of one moving flame against dying twilight.
LOCATION MAP
Dense forest at deep blue hour, minutes after sunset. Camera retreats backward along a rough forest path, facing the man. Foreground: the flame, thin drifting smoke, and one blurred fern at the frame edge. Midground: the man walking toward camera over uneven ground of roots and leaf litter. Background: black vertical tree trunks layered into depth, thin ground mist between them, and gaps in the canopy showing a deep twilight-blue sky gradient, darkest overhead, faintly brighter low behind him. The torch is the only warm light; the sky gives only a weak cold ambient.
FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING
The first visible frame already contains the man walking with the torch burning and the blue twilight visible behind him; no empty establishing frame, no delayed reveal.
The man: mid-thirties, lean and weathered, dark rugged expedition clothing, alert and focused. Frontal, midground at x 50%, y 55%, framed chest-up, torso facing camera, walking straight toward the lens at a slow steady pace, eyes scanning the darkness past the camera.
The torch: gripped in HIS RIGHT hand, which appears screen-left. Flame starts at head height, x 38%, y 40%, about 40 cm from his face. The flame stays inside the frame for the entire shot.
Because the flame starts on his right side, the right side of his face is lit warm and the left side falls into shadow with only a trace of cool blue edge — this left-right logic must stay physically correct all shot.
Foreground occlusion: a dark blurred fern at FG lower-right, x 88%, y 85%, heavily defocused for the full shot.
Background: tree trunks as blue-black silhouettes between x 10% and x 90%, sky gaps glowing dim twilight blue in the upper third.
FORMAT MODE
Single continuous take. Real-time speed. No cuts, no transitions, no speed ramps.
OPTICS
84° diagonal field of view, classic wide-angle lens character, camera 1.2 to 1.5 meters from the man. Strong but natural perspective, straight lines rectilinear, no fisheye curve. His face is sharp and dominant; the flame blooms soft; the forest recedes into layered blue-black depth. Subtle horizontal flare streak from the flame, gentle optical vignette.
CAMERA
Handheld, eye-level, frontal. The operator walks backward at the man's exact pace, holding the 1.2–1.5 meter distance for all 10 seconds: shoulder-mounted mass, visible breathing sway, soft footstep pulse, small human corrections — organic and alive, never digital jitter, never gimbal-smooth. Focus stays locked on his face for the entire shot; the flame may bloom but never steals focus.
ACTION TIMING
0.0s to 2.5s — Full staging from frame one. He walks toward camera, torch raised beside his right cheek at x 38%, y 40%. The right side of his face glows warm and bright; the left side sits in shadow with a faint cold blue trace; the dividing line runs down his nose. Firelight flickers on his skin in rhythm with the flame.
2.5s to 4.5s — He extends his right arm forward to inspect something ahead: the flame moves toward the camera, growing larger in frame at x 40%, y 45%, and AWAY from his face — the warm light on his face visibly drops and he dims toward a cool blue-edged silhouette against the twilight, while nearby trunks and mist flare warm.
4.5s to 7.0s — He sweeps the torch in an arc across his body to his left side: the flame crosses frame from x 40% to x 62%. As it passes his face the light surges bright for a beat, then the lighting flips — now the LEFT side of his face is lit warm and the right side falls to shadow with its faint blue edge. Long tree shadows rotate through the mist opposite the flame's travel.
7.0s to 8.5s — He pulls the torch back in close, within 25 cm of his left cheek at x 55%, y 38%: light intensity on his skin jumps to its brightest, pores and sweat visible, both eyes catching warm glints, the blue ambient disappearing from his face entirely.
8.5s to 10.0s — Still walking, he lowers the torch to chest height at x 45%, y 58%: the face is now underlit from below, brow and nose shadows invert upward, the top of his head falls to cold blue-black. He keeps moving toward camera, breath fogging warm through the glow.
PHYSICS
Light on the face obeys the flame physically at every moment: closer flame means brighter skin, farther flame means dimmer skin, and the lit side of the face is always the side the flame is on — warm light falls off fast with distance, and where it dies, only the faint blue ambient remains. The flame behaves like real fire: it leans against the arm's motion, stretches on fast sweeps, flutters in night air, recovers when the arm settles; embers detach and drift upward; heat shimmer bends the air above it; thin smoke trails the moving flame. The torch has mass — his wrist and forearm visibly carry and steer it with acceleration and deceleration. His steps land with real weight: leaf litter compresses, twigs snap, roots shift his balance. Ground mist stirs as he passes. Breath vapor drifts in the cold air.
LIGHTING
Two light sources with a strict hierarchy. KEY: the hand-held torch flame — warm orange-amber, flickering, moving; the entire lighting of his face is driven by the flame's position, distance and height, changing continuously and naturally. AMBIENT: faint blue-hour skylight, many times weaker than the torch — a cold blue wash that only separates tree silhouettes from black, breathes a dim gradient into the sky gaps, and leaves the thinnest cool edge on his shoulders, hair and the shadow side of his face; it never fills his features, never competes with the flame. The shot is exposed for the flame: the forest holds deep crushed shadow, no fill light beyond the sky's trace, no flat front light. Halation blooms around the flame; the mist carries warm glow near him and cold blue farther away.
AUDIO
Close torch crackle and flame whoosh on every sweep, footsteps pressing into leaf litter, an occasional twig snap, low night wind moving the canopy, one distant owl call, his controlled breathing. No dialogue, lips still, no subtitles, no score.
POSITIVE LOCKS
The torch is the only warm light for all 10 seconds and the light on his face tracks the flame position at every frame; the blue ambient stays faint and cold. Focus stays locked on his face for all 10 seconds. He walks toward camera and the camera retreats with him for the full duration. Kodak Vision3 500T tungsten texture: dense organic film grain, pronounced halation around the flame, crushed blacks with a soft toe, photochemical palette of deep orange against deep twilight blue. IMAX-scale clarity on his face, Panavision-style anamorphic character: oval bokeh and subtle horizontal flare. Lubezki-style natural-light handheld realism — the frame looks like a real archival film shot, not digital video.
```

Shoulder-mounted mass throughout — organic and alive, never digital jitter, never gimbal-smooth. Focus stays locked on his face for the entire shot; the window may bloom but never steals focus.
ACTION TIMING
0.0s to 2.5s — Full staging from frame one: he sits in the dim depth of the room, mug in hand, steam rising from the coffee, eyes on the window. He leans forward, plants his feet, and rises off the chair — the chair creaks, his weight settles onto his legs. The camera rises with him.
