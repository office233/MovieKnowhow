# Who owns my generations, and can I use them commercially?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/account/who-owns-my-generations-and-can-i-use-them-commercially
Published: Aug 2, 2026 (4 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (rights). **You own inputs and outputs** (ToS 4.4); commercial use unrestricted for all users (ads, client work, products, YouTube monetization, selling outputs); no extra license to buy. Higgsfield gets a hosting/processing license (ends on deletion, with narrow exceptions) and may train on content (stop by deleting; Enterprise excluded). Restrictions: do not use outputs to train other AI models; no contests/sweepstakes run through the service without consent; you must not infringe copyright/trademark/likeness and need permission to upload another person's image. IP indemnification only on Enterprise. Free-plan outputs carry a watermark.
