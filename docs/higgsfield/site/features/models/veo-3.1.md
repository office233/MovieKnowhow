# Google Veo 3.1 on Higgsfield

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/veo3.1 (crawled 2026-09). Marketing/model page, no example prompts.

## Specs (from page widget + FAQ)
- Default widget: Resolution 1080p, Duration 8s.
- Resolutions: 720p or 1080p at 24 fps. Durations: 4, 6 or 8 s only. Aspect: 16:9 or 9:16 only.
- Two variants:
  - **Veo 3.1 (Standard)** - text-to-video and Multi-Image Reference mode; best quality and subject consistency; supports talking characters with lip-sync (dialogue).
  - **Veo 3.1 Fast** - text-to-video and Start & End Frame mode; faster, better for controlled motion.
- Three generation modes:
  1. **Start & End Frame** - upload 2 images (first + last frame); model builds a coherent transition between them (Fast model).
  2. **Multi-Image Reference** - 1-3 reference images to lock a character/object identity or guide composition (Standard only; called "Subject Consistency").
  3. **Text-to-video**.

## How to use it (practical)
- Need a talking character or a recurring hero object -> Standard + 1-3 references.
- Need a controlled move between two known frames (product reveal, before/after, match transition) -> Fast + start/end frames.
- 8 s is the ceiling: plan shots in 4/6/8 s beats and stitch in an editor.
- Elsewhere on the site Veo 3.1 is recommended for "character consistency and 4K" (long-video page FAQ) and "top-tier realism and reference-driven control".
