## Takeaways for film / ad / UGC production (derived from the data above)

1. **Seedance 2.0 is the workhorse**: ~67% of all generations across community projects; 5 projects are >85% Seedance. Stills (Soul Cinematic, Soul V2, Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image 2.0, Seedream) are used as keyframes/reference boards, then animated. Seedance 2.5 appears only in the newest project (Dusk, Aug 2026, 35%).
2. **Image-first pipeline**: heavy "Soul Cinematic" (47–52% in 4K Blockbuster Breakdown and REWIND CLUB) plus Cinematic Studio Soul Cast / Soul Location entries show the workflow: lock cast and locations as stills in Cinema Studio, then image-to-video.
3. **Scope format for premium films**: nearly all Higgsfield Studio features master at 4096×1716 (2.39:1) — Cully Hill Boys, Hell Grind, Oneiric, Kok Boru, The Trigger, 4K Blockbuster. Ads/UGC use 16:9 1080p–1440p; one music video uses 21:9 3440×1440.
4. **Budget the iteration**: Higgsfield Studio films burn 10–70 generations per finished second (Cully Hill Boys: 473k generations for a ~115-min film; Oneiric ~35/s; The Trigger ~10/s); solo community creators ship at ~2–10 generations/sec, and polished "breakdown" pieces at 30–67/s. Plan coverage/retakes accordingly.
5. **Credit costs**: most community projects land at ~45–380 credits per generation (image-heavy projects are cheap: Soul Carousels ~2/gen, REWIND CLUB ~11/gen); 4K Seedance video work sits at the top (Cat story ~344/gen, Huntress's Tale ~379/gen, Gothic Beach ~1,300/gen). A 3.4-minute 4K blockbuster breakdown cost ~937k credits; a 106 s sports ad ~55k.
6. **Short-form lengths that ship**: ads 60–105 s, launch videos 45–65 s, cinematic teasers 15–25 s, multi-part series parts 55–200 s; studio shorts 2–20 min; features 96–115 min.
7. **Series structure**: Originals release an Episode 1 / Trailer first; later episodes get a small fraction of the views (Arena Zero ep1 1.22M vs ep5 942), so front-load the hook in ep1/trailer. Casts are defined as named character cards (e.g. MORK: Jarl, Kael, Bjorn…).
8. **Contest → Originals funnel**: winners of "Make Your Action Scene" (judges PJ Ace, Jason Zada, Yelaman) were praised for concept + comedic payoff, editing rhythm, realism and distinctive stylization — the bar for being picked for an Original Series.
