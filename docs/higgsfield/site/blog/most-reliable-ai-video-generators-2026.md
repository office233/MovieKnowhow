## Notes

**Topic:** reliability testing across 15 prompt categories (Jul 22 2026). Thesis: reliability = right model for the task + explicit production parameters (director panel) that remove the model's interpretive latitude. The least predictable outputs came from prompts that left genre, lighting and camera style to inference.

**Model roles:** Kling 3.0 — human subjects, micro-expressions, up to 6 connected scenes per pass; Veo 3.1 — final delivery, highest fidelity + native audio (no conversational editing, so get the prompt right first); Seedance 2.0 — commercial multi-reference (up to 9 inputs: character, location, product, style, audio); Cinema Studio 3.5 — director panel applied at generation time. A preset is a hard constraint; the same idea written as text is a suggestion.

**Default "epic" recipe used for most tests:** Seedance 2.0 + Genre Epic, Lighting Contre-jour, Palette Teal & Orange Epic, MoveSet Epic Scale, Camera Fine Film, Lens Anamorphic, 35mm, f/4, 16:9 (9:16 for social). Why it works: Contre-jour turns subjects into rim-lit shapes and stays consistent across 6-11 hard cuts; Epic Scale holds one camera logic (locked close beats + one committed track/crane/whip) instead of resetting per beat; Seedance carries physical continuity (smoke, tears, straps, falling props) across cuts.
**Manual Style examples** (named looks written into the prompt instead of presets): Noir — "Chiaroscuro Hard Key", High-Contrast B&W, Slow Push-In, Spherical 50mm f/2.8; UGC beauty — Soft Window Daylight, Clean Warm Neutrals, Handheld Selfie, Clean Digital, Spherical 24mm f/2.0 (keeps product size/label and cap state consistent); Luxury ketchup reveal — Dramatic Studio Rim Light, Deep Black & Gold, Slow Orbit + Push-In, Macro Spherical 85mm f/2.0 (Clean Digital, no grain fighting glossy CGI).
**Prompt skeleton used:** `GLOBAL STYLE:` line (photoreal, Fine Film grain, grade, motivated light only) -> numbered beats/hard cuts with camera per beat -> physics/continuity notes -> audio.
Categories tested: dramatic exterior, fantasy action (dragons), commercial product (MP3 player in NYC), cosmic landscape (black hole), noir, multi-character (paratroopers), griffin chase, UGC sunscreen ad, horror (school guard), ketchup CGI, documentary pull-back reveal, sci-fi fleet, fashion (Grok Imagine 1.5), corporate/garage, music video.
