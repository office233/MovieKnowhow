# How do Credit Packs work?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/credits/how-credit-packs-work
Published: Aug 3, 2026 (2 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (credits). Credit Pack = one-time extra credits; needs an active subscription to buy; no plan change, no renewal. Sizes: fixed 100/200/500/1,000/2,000/4,000; adjustable 5,000-25,000 in 1,000 steps (prices at checkout). Valid 90 days. Spend order: subscription credits first, then other balances by soonest expiry. Survives cancellation (usable on free-plan models until expiry). Refundable within 7 days if none of the pack's credits were used.
