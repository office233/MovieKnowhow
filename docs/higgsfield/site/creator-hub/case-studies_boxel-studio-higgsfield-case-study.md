# How Boxel Studio, the Team Behind Netflix and Hulu Hits, Brought AI Into Pre-production With Higgsfield

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/case-studies/boxel-studio-higgsfield-case-study
Published: Jun 30, 2026 (4 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: customer story (Boxel Studio, 350+ artist animation/VFX studio, US/Canada; clients Netflix, Hulu, FX, Blumhouse, Mattel). Tools: Cinema Studio (plus Seedance 2.0, Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1 on the same subscription).

**Use**: AI confined to pre-production and look development: rapid environment prototypes, sequence visualization, alternate visual styles, early storytelling exploration for new concepts.

**Results**: time-to-visualization from months to days; fewer iteration cycles and less downstream rework when direction changes; more options shown to clients earlier; artists focus on decisions instead of technical bottlenecks.

**Policy points**: human artists own original designs, direction and final execution ("human-centric" AI). Enterprise terms mattered: uploaded data not used for training, strict project-data lifecycle and permanent deletion.

**Takeaway for filmmakers**: use Cinema Studio as a previz/lookdev engine to lock direction with stakeholders before expensive production.
