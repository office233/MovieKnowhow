- **Similarity Score** (free, "AI content IP checker"): scores images, videos and audio for resemblance to protected IP before you publish - a pre-flight check for contest entries and ads (both contest rulebooks ban third-party IP).
- Mix-up to know: the slug **video-rehex** is the **Color Palette** app (recolour a video from a preset palette or one extracted from any reference image) while **video-relight** is **Relight** (change lighting while keeping detail). Their SEO titles are swapped.
- Similarity Score's "Explore more apps" rail: Virality Predictor (score a hook before posting), Expand image, Angles 2.0 (any angle of an image), Shots (9 shots from one image), Transitions, Color grading (free), What's Next? (8 narrative continuations of one scene), Behind the Scenes, Zooms (9 zoom levels), Skin Enhancer.
- /apps/link-to-video-ad and /marketing-studio(/favorites, /generations, /product) return the **Marketing Studio** shell: "Turn any product into ready to post content"; template tabs All / Images / Videos and categories Product shot, Motion, UGC, Ads, Posters, Marketplace; tools **Ad Reference** and **Product Link** (paste a product URL -> video ad); sections Home, My generations, My favorites, Projects.
