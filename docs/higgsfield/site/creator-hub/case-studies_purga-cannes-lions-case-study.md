# Purga, Cannes Lions Winner, Builds AI Film Worlds with Higgsfield

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/case-studies/purga-cannes-lions-case-study
Published: Sep 8, 2026 (5 mins read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: customer story (PURGA, AI creative studio, Buenos Aires, founded 2025). Cannes Lions 2026 Bronze Lion (Film) for "Lorem Ipsum"; 2 shortlists. Tool: Cinema Studio (characters, shots, imagery); animation and music done in other AI tools.

**Core technique: characters first, then the world**
- Problem: one great image is easy; twenty images that belong to the same film (same characters, look, intention) is hard across fragmented tools.
- Fix the characters and visual direction first; then explore shots around them. Casting, lenses, framing and camera position become decisions within one visual system instead of being re-solved per image.
- Consistency is maintained as you go, not recovered afterwards.
- Iteration speed lets you test alternative directions even in a defined project; clients explore variations while direction is still open.

**Philosophy**: "artisanal" AI; value = control; time saved on mechanics is reinvested in idea, direction, craft.
