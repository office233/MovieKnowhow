# How One of One, the Studio Behind the Official Maradona Label, Took a Brand World From Concept to Sold Out With Higgsfield

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/case-studies/one-of-one-studio-interview
Published: Jul 14, 2026 (9 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: customer story / interview (One of One, AI-native sports & entertainment studio; official Maradona apparel label under Electa Global license). Tools: Soul ID, Cinema Studio, Nano Banana Pro, Nano Banana 2, Seedance 2.0, Kling 3.0.

**Numbers**: first collection sold out in 3 weeks; 10M+ people reached; brand world delivered in weeks instead of months; no location travel (Buenos Aires, Naples atmospheres generated).

**Workflow**
1. Creative development: visualize concepts as static images and present a visual storyboard, not a written treatment; iterate fast on client feedback until direction is locked.
2. For talent/likeness work, agree up front what must stay true to the person (look, movement, feel) and treat it as the rulebook for every asset.
3. Execution with model routing: Nano Banana Pro / Nano Banana 2 / Cinema Studio for concept art, campaign, editorial and e-commerce stills (consistency across the collection); Seedance 2.0 and Kling 3.0 for campaign films and animation.
4. Finish in-platform: upscaling, sound, assembly.

**Lessons**
- Build from real culture/places/symbols around the IP rather than inventing mythology.
- Hard part is one consistent visual language across web, social, press, product, films, editorial.
- Stills are past the quality bar; video is improving but choose carefully which shots clear the bar.
- Emerging asset class: licensed AI rights to a person's likeness (content without talent on set).
- "Models are instruments, the platform is the orchestra"; taste is the differentiator.
