# How do I join a partnership or creator program?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/business/how-do-i-join-a-partnership-or-creator-program
Published: Aug 2, 2026 (3 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (programs).
- **Higgsfield Earn** (creators.higgsfield.ai): connect Instagram (auto) or YouTube/X (verification code in bio); accept matched paid campaigns (format, platform, hashtags, min length); guaranteed payout + performance bonus about a week later; payouts via Garna; regional availability; rates and communications confidential; one appeal per rejected submission.
- **Creator Partnership Program (CPP)**: apply on the CPP page (platforms, audience, country, recent work; no follower minimum; human review). Partners get a monthly full plan, extra credits for big projects, early model access, direct team line, partner community; must tag/mention Higgsfield.
- **B2B/studio**: Pricing -> Business plans -> Contact Sales (Enterprise). Brand/ambassador inquiries: marketing@higgsfield.ai.
