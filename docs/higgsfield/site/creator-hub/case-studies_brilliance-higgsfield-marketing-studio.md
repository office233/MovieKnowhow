# Brilliance Cut Production Costs by 80% with Marketing Studio

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/case-studies/brilliance-higgsfield-marketing-studio
Published: Aug 28, 2026 (5 mins read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: customer story (Brilliance, online engagement-ring/diamond retailer). Tools: GPT Image 2, Nano Banana Pro, Marketing Studio.

**Numbers**: photo-shoot-grade campaign ~$100k -> ~$10k plus labor (labor + software ~ $15k/month across the operation); production cost -80%; turnaround 6-8 days -> 3-4 days; output 5-10x; 2-3 campaigns run in parallel. Most imagery on Brilliance.com is now generated.

**Techniques**
- Route each asset to the cheapest engine that is good enough: distant/high-volume shots do not need the most expensive model; reserve premium models for close-ups.
- Most volume = still images + short video; Marketing Studio is replacing part of UGC content.
- Pipeline automations link Higgsfield with Notion, Google Drive and Claude; automated high-volume retouching pipelines.
- Heavy QC for product truth: ring width, taper, prong and facet geometry, stone shine must match the physical item; every frame gets Photoshop finishing; regenerate as many times as needed when geometry is off.

**Pitfall**: jewelry close-ups are not push-button; budget QC labor. Frame savings as cost-efficiency (more output per dollar) rather than "cheaper".
