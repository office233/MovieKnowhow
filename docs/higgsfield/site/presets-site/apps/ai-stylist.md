## What it does

Your personal AI Stylist. Upload your own clothes or mix and match from presets. Change poses, backgrounds, and try on complete outfits instantly.

**Virtual try-on / "AI fitting room" (custom tool).** Pick a model, then mix and match items from the preset library or upload photos of your own clothes (even design sketches). You can swap single items (e.g. only the top) or the whole look, and also change **pose and background**. Use it to preview garments for e-commerce, lookbooks or outfit planning.
