# How VertexCGI, the Studio Behind Campaigns for Porsche and Mercedes, Used Higgsfield to Build a Viral NBA Finals Campaign

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/case-studies/vertexcgi-higgsfield-nba-finals-case-study
Published: Jul 28, 2026 (6 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: customer story (Vertex CGI, "attention company": 25B+ organic views, 1,200+ campaigns, 200+ clients incl. Nike, Mercedes, Porsche). Project: NBA Finals champion celebration video with ESPN, picked up by SportsCenter. Tools: Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image 2 (concept), Kling 3.0 (production).

**Timeline**: 5 days from first call to publish, while preparing directions for both finalists (Knicks and Spurs) because the winner was unknown until the end.

**Process**
1. Strategy before production: research audiences, platform behavior, culture, why people share. Build a creative blueprint (key visual moments, pacing, structure).
2. Map every audience (ESPN, NBA, winning team, New Yorkers, Instagram, general basketball fans) and design for the intersection: Easter eggs for die-hards, cultural references for fans, cinematic spectacle for casual viewers.
3. Generate visuals live during the client call ("is this what you mean?") to align instantly instead of mood boards/storyboard artists.
4. Concept/storyboard stills with Nano Banana Pro and GPT Image 2 (compositions, angles, directions).
5. After the storyboard lock, Kling 3.0 is the main video model.
6. **Draft at 720p, final at 1080p**: iterate camera movement, pacing, prompts at 720p (speed over pixels), re-render only winning shots at 1080p for the edit.
7. Traditional tools (Photoshop, After Effects) for precise fixes.

**Other modes they use**: shoot live footage and use AI for texturing, tracking, masking, environment replacement; mix filmed and generated; or fully generated sequences.
