# Why didn’t I receive a refund after canceling my subscription?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/refunds/why-didnt-i-receive-a-refund-after-canceling-my-subscription
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (2 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (refunds). Canceling and refunding are separate: canceling only stops renewal. Request a refund separately if still eligible (7 days, no credits used). EEA/UK consumers may have a 14-day withdrawal window.
