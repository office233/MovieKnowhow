# How do I use AI Influencer?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/tools/how-do-i-use-ai-influencer
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (3 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (tool guide). AI Influencer Studio = menu-driven character builder (no prompting), output up to 4K. Open via AI Influencer in top nav or higgsfield.ai/ai-influencer-studio -> Create new.

- **Builder panel (base identity)**: Character Type (human, mammal, reptile, fish, hybrid, alien; categories can be blended), Gender, Ethnicity/Origin (single or mixed), Skin Color, Eye Color, Skin Conditions (freckles, scars, birthmarks, vitiligo, albinism...), Age.
- **Advanced settings**: Face (eye type/details, mouth & teeth, ears, horns, skin material, surface pattern), Body (type, height, proportions, extra limbs for non-humans), Style (hair, accessories, rendering style). Reset clears all.
- Generate (cost shown) -> regenerate / save, or type a change in "Type to customize character" and press Edit (costs credits). Saved characters live in the library on the left.
- **Animate**: video button under preview; for performance-driven motion record yourself and use Motion Control (character image + your recording); or movement templates. Talking heads via Lipsync.
- **Reuse across tools**: generate a batch of portraits, train a Soul ID on 20+ of them (works in Soul 2.0 and Soul Cinema); scale with Supercomputer.
