Ownership and use of your work (Terms s.4)
- Higgsfield claims **no ownership** of inputs or outputs and does not restrict commercial use; rights in exported outputs **survive cancellation or account deletion** and can be transferred/sublicensed to clients.
- You must hold rights and **consents for any real person's name, likeness or voice** in your inputs (including yourself).
- **Training**: your content, inputs and outputs may be used to train Higgsfield models; you stop it going forward by deleting the content or account. **Enterprise agreements exclude training** and treat content as confidential. (The Photoshop plugin FAQ says images "aren't used for training" - the Terms are the governing text.)
- Content in non-private areas can be credited to your username; other users may see and reuse what you make public.

Storage (s.3): Higgsfield need not store your content; retention may vary by plan and old outputs may move to **cold storage**; accounts with no login, no generation and no subscription for **30 days** can be marked inactive and their content moved to cold storage after notice.

Moderation (s.6): outputs may carry invisible **C2PA / watermark provenance**; NCII removed within 48 h; if told content infringes, you must stop using it and not regenerate substantially the same output.

Money (s.9-10)
- Refund: within **7 days** of initial purchase and only if **no credits used**; service fee up to 6%; renewals not refundable; upgrade refunds only for the increment. Add-on/pack credits valid **90 days**, consumed after subscription credits; subscription credits never roll over.
- Price increases announced **30 days** ahead and apply from the next term; failed payments get a ~14-day cure window.
- **"Unlimited" plans are subject to fair use**: separate slower queue, throttling of automated or far-above-typical use, no guaranteed speed or concurrency.
- Free trials/promos auto-convert to paid at standard price unless cancelled.

Disputes (s.18): binding individual arbitration and class-action waiver unless you opt out within 30 days.

Privacy Policy highlights: face/voice uploads may involve **biometric data**, used only transiently for the requested feature and not stored or sold; prompts, inputs and outputs may be used to improve/train models; removed content may stay on active servers up to 30 days plus backups; separate sections for US-state and EU/UK rights (contact privacy@higgsfield.ai).

Cookie Notice: four categories - Essential, Analytics & Customization, Performance & Functionality, Advertising - with preference controls.
