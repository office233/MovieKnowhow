Details worth re-stating because they are easy to miss:
- Pricing quoted at ~20 credits per dollar for a **15-second** clip: 720p **30 credits (~$1.50)**, 1080p **37.5 (~$1.88)**, 4K **90 (~$4.50)**. Multi-shot costs the same as single-shot of the same total length.
- Five common mistakes: overloading one shot (one action + one camera move per shot), prompting motion that cannot reach the attached end frame, too many shots for the duration, expecting motion transfer from base Kling 3.0 (use Kling Motion Control 3.0), and iterating in 4K (draft at 720p, then rerun the final).
