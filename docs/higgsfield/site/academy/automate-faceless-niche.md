URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/automate-faceless-niche/picking-a-topic-with-live-research

- Don't brainstorm the topic yourself - ask the skill one question; it **researches the live web, scores candidates**, returns a short list and picks one (faceless 10-min explainers).

## Lesson 4 — Writing a Script That Stays Monetized

URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/automate-faceless-niche/writing-the-monetizable-script

- Two layers: **prompts** carry the visuals; the **script** carries meaning - without it you get "beautiful nonsense".
- Monetization: YouTube doesn't auto-demonetize AI content; it flags spam/unoriginal content regardless of AI. A genuinely good script is what keeps the channel monetized.
- In Adil's tests **Claude Fable 5** produced the strongest scripts/prompts, which showed up in view counts.
- Retention-first structure: (1) hook opens on the payoff, not backstory; (2) chapters that each set up the next; (3) an **open loop about every minute** (a dangling question).

## Lesson 5 — Generating the Full 10-Minute Video

URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/automate-faceless-niche/generating-the-full-video

- Generation is one more message in the same chat; Higgsfield renders the whole film from that prompt.
- One decision: length. The skill recommends **10 minutes** (banks watch time, which YouTube rewards) as long as it stays interesting.
- Example: Pyramid of Giza video, **~100 scenes in one consistent style**, shot changes every few seconds (same pacing as big channels).
- The real achievement is coherence: one narrator, one style, one idea across 10 minutes without shots drifting.

## Lesson 7 — Cloning Your Own Voice

URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/automate-faceless-niche/cloning-your-own-voice

- Narrate in **your own voice**. Takes ~1 minute: higgsfield.ai -> **Audio -> Voice Presets -> "Create a custom voice"** -> record the sample script shown -> upload. Saved as a preset for any future render.
- Benefits: a voice nobody else on YouTube has, and a signal that a real specific person made it -> less likely to be flagged as AI-generated.

## Lesson 9 — 20 Shorts in One Click

URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/automate-faceless-niche/twenty-shorts-in-one-click

- Same chat -> **Shorts Studio** -> ~20 cut, captioned, ready-to-post shorts from the long video, no manual editing.
- Why: a 10-minute video asks for a commitment; a short needs none.
- Monetization math: YouTube Partner needs **1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours**. A 10-min video watched 4 min on average needs ~**60,000 views** total (across all uploads) to reach 4,000 h.
- Post the same batch to **YouTube Shorts, Reels and TikTok**; every clip points back to the channel.

## Lesson 10 — The 30-Day Content Plan

URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/automate-faceless-niche/the-30-day-content-plan

- Ask for the month, not the next video: **8 long videos ranked by search volume**, each paired with its shorts batch, backed by search data.
- The plan is runnable: "start videos two and three" renders both **in parallel**.
- Rules: post on a schedule you can keep - **2 long videos/week, shorts daily, same days and time**; watch **average view duration** - drop-off points are notes for the next script (did your open loops work?).
