# How do credits work?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/credits/how-credits-work
Published: Aug 3, 2026 (4 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (credits). Cost depends on model, resolution, duration; always shown on the Generate button.
| Credit type | Source | Resets | Expires |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subscription | plan | each cycle | unused expire at renewal (no rollover) |
| Credit Pack | one-time buy | no | 90 days |
| Auto-Refill | automatic top-up | no | 90 days |
| Promo | codes/compensation | no | date shown |
| Boost | Concurrency Boost packs | no | 90 days |
- Spend order: subscription first, then soonest-expiring.
- Credits are charged on web, MCP, CLI, Canvas, Supercomputer for images, videos, re-rolls, upscales. Free: browsing, Unlimited-model and plan free generations **on higgsfield.ai only**.
- Monthly plans reset when renewal invoice is paid; annual plans reset every 30 days (date drifts: May 11 -> Jun 10).
- Usage log: Manage Account -> Usage (per-generation deductions, refunds, per-model breakdown). Failed generations usually auto-refund within minutes (some models excepted).
