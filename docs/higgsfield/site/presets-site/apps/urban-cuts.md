## What it does

Urban Cuts AI tool — generate next-gen beat-synced outfit transition videos. Perfect for TikTok fashion content and viral Reels. Create yours free.

**Beat-synced outfit video (stepper tool, routing key `beatfit`).** Steps: (1) upload a character photo plus a clear image of the outfit; (2) choose a track from the curated music presets or upload your own (the beat sets the cut points); (3) generate. Output is a **10-second** video whose cuts land on detected music beats. Uploaded clothes are auto-isolated from their background before compositing. Default aspect is **9:16**, with 3:4, 1:1, 4:3 and 16:9 also available. Aimed at designers and resellers who need fashion reels for TikTok/Reels.
