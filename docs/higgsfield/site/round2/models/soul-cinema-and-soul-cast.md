## Soul Cinema (image model)
- Film-still look: rich textures, natural grain, skin and material depth, bold compositions and unexpected angles; "era-aware" (knows what the '70s, '90s and today look like).
- Start from text or a reference image.
- Pairs with **Soul ID** (same face across scenes) and **Soul HEX** (exact palette / brand colour control).
- Use it for: keyframes and storyboards before video generation, mood frames for music videos, campaign concept frames, gallery artwork.

## Soul Cast (AI actor builder for Cinema Studio)
- "Build, don't describe": instead of writing a face prompt you set parameters - **genre, era, archetype, physique, outfit, imperfections, budget slider** (raw/authentic -> polished/premium) - and get a cast member with an auto-written **backstory** ("a full casting sheet").
- Identity holds from wide shots to close-ups inside Cinema Studio (2.0 and later).
- Use cases: full cast without casting budget; era-matched faces for music videos; audience-matched faces for brand campaigns; several distinct casts per day for agencies (e.g. "formal Everyman" vs "high-fashion Rebel"); one consistent presenter across hundreds of e-learning modules.
- Workflow: define each character once in Soul Cast -> reuse as an element in every Cinema Studio shot -> keep wardrobe/imperfection settings fixed for continuity.
