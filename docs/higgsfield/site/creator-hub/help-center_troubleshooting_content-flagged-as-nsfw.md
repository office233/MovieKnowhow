# Why was my content flagged as NSFW, and what can I do if it was wrong?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/troubleshooting/content-flagged-as-nsfw
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (2 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (troubleshooting). Two filter layers: Higgsfield (prompt stage + upload/reference stage) and external providers (Nano Banana, Seedream, Kling, Sora 2, MiniMax, Wan, Veo). False-positive triggers: swimwear/revealing clothes, fitness, medical/anatomical, borderline reference composition, ambiguous words. Fixes: neutral specific rephrasing; swap/remove the reference; add a descriptive text prompt to image-only inputs; try another model (different provider filter). Credits usually auto-refunded; classifier decisions cannot be overridden.
