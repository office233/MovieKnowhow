## What it does

AI meme generator — create viral memes from any photo in seconds. Trending formats, custom text, instant sharing. Generate your meme free.

**Free meme builder with AI characters (made with Soul Cinema).** Steps: (1) pick a ready-made character preset or type a prompt to generate a new photoreal person; (2) add caption text and drag/resize it anywhere; (3) optionally hit **Generate text** to get **5 AI caption options** matched to the image; (4) download in high quality with no watermark. Characters are fully synthetic, so the page describes them as IP-safe with no likeness-rights issues, and commercial use is allowed. You can browse without an account, but generating characters from a prompt, AI captions and downloads need a free account.
