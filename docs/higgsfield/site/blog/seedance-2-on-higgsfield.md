- Evaluation is shifting from single impressive frames to **sequence-level coherence**: stable motion, consistent framing, continuity over longer shots.
- Key interest areas: **camera logic** (pans, tracking, controlled reveals; sustained direction and depth), motion stability good enough for editing/compositing, temporal stability of lighting/textures/spatial relationships (slow shots expose inconsistencies that fast cuts hide), persistent character identity, macro/physics shots as stress tests (texture stability, subtle motion) — important for product visuals.
- Compared with Seedance 1.5 (baseline for coherent short-form), 2.0 emphasises temporal control and deliberate camera behaviour.
- Practical takeaway: treat generated clips as workflow layers (combine with motion design, typography, timing) rather than final one-offs.
