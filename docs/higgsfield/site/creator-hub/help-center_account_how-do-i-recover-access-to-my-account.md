# How do I recover access to my account?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/account/how-do-i-recover-access-to-my-account
Published: Aug 2, 2026 (2 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (account). Google/Apple/Microsoft logins recover via the provider; email accounts via Forgot password (6-digit code). Email cannot be changed; credits/subscriptions/generations cannot be transferred between accounts. Support verifies ownership using account/billing details (never password or full card).
