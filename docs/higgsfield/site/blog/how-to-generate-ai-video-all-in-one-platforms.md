## Notes

**Topic:** a 10-step all-in-one workflow compared across Higgsfield, Runway, Kling, Dreamina CapCut, LTX Studio (Jun 28 2026). No actual prompt examples (the page has a placeholder where examples were planned).

**10 steps (platform-agnostic):**
1. Brief: purpose, who appears, what happens, where published — decide aspect ratio first (9:16 TikTok/Shorts/Reels, 16:9 LinkedIn/YouTube).
2. Character: decide real face vs AI face vs avatar before generating. Higgsfield Soul ID (20+ photos, cross-model), Runway Director Mode (per session), Kling reference-based (drifts across very different scenes), Dreamina none, LTX Elements.
3. Prompt order: subject + action -> setting + lighting -> camera move -> mood. Camera terms understood widely: dolly in, truck left, arc shot, push in, pull back wide, handheld follow, crane up, orbital move.
4. Model on Higgsfield: Seedance 2.0 (commercial, multi-reference), Veo 3.1 (cinematic realism + native audio), Kling 3.0 (stylised multi-shot), WAN 2.6 (frame-level control), Hailuo 2.3 (fast iteration). Kling Professional mode ≈ 3.5x credits.
5. Generate at **720p first** (1080p ~2x cost); change one thing at a time.
6. Watch the whole clip — problems show at 5-8 s; check the face in seconds 3-7 of a 10 s clip.
7. Re-run the identical prompt at final resolution (Higgsfield up to 4K on Seedance 2.0 / Veo 3.1).
8. Audio: native on Veo 3.1/Seedance 2.0 (add voice/music/ambience via **@audio**), LipSync Studio 8+ languages.
9. Assemble: 30-60 s video = **4-8 clips**; use Seedance first-and-last-frame to generate transition shots; edit in your NLE.
10. Export to platform specs (TikTok/Reels vertical <60 s; LinkedIn/X 16:9 or 1:1); Marketing Studio can output platform-formatted ad assets from a URL.
