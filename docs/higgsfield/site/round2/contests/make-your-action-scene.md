# Make Your Action Scene (Higgsfield contest, Feb-Mar 2026)

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/contests/make-your-action-scene (data read from the 23 submission pages crawled in round 2; each page embeds the full contest object).

## Facts

- Theme: an action scene of any kind (martial arts, military, spy, superhero, post-apocalyptic). **Any model allowed** (Seedance, Kling and others).
- Window: submissions Feb 9 (11:00 PT) - Feb 28, 2026 (23:59 PT); judging Feb 28 - Mar 17; winners announced Mar 17. Status: finished.
- **8,752 projects submitted.** Prize pool $500,000: 1st $150k, 2nd $100k, 3rd $50k, up to 20 honorable mentions at $10k each (all 20 were awarded). Paid within 30 days.
- Entry rules (paraphrased): 18+; up to 10 entries per person; free; 15 s to 5 min; in English; must carry the Higgsfield watermark; no copyrighted IP, porn, violence against real people, politics or religion; must be posted publicly on at least one social network with a link to Higgsfield and **#HiggsfieldAction**; posting to r/HiggsfieldAI was optional; entries editable until the deadline.

## Judging criteria (weights from the rules)

| Criterion | Weight | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Cinematic quality | 25% | polish, camera work, lighting, grade - does it look like a real action scene |
| Storytelling & creativity | 25% | concept, structure, pacing, originality |
| Technical execution | 20% | purposeful use of the AI tools, asset integration, consistency |
| Platform engagement | 15% | views, likes and clones inside the Higgsfield contest gallery |
| Social media engagement | 15% | reach and interaction on the public social post |

30% of the score was audience reach - unlike the [Global Film Festival](global-film-festival.md), where the jury ignores social performance.

## What the winning films have in common (lessons for your own contest film)

1. **A single, pitchable comic or emotional premise beats spectacle.** The 1st-place film is a breakfast-table war between a grandmother and a wasp; the jury note is one word of praise for concept and execution. Others that placed: grandma-and-grandkids heist (What A Lovely Day), sisters fighting over a last bao, crash-test dummies escaping (SCRATCH, 3rd), an ant's dream.
2. **Genre pastiche with a clear visual language** gets noticed: grindhouse trailer (HARVEST SEASON), 80s VHS fantasy (FROST BLOOD 3), 1978 analog spy world (PALE SIGNAL), Art Deco dieselpunk boxing (The Brass Bastards), Indian masala action (Kaliyug Rising), mockumentary real-estate review (Who The F*ck Is Olga?).
3. **Judges repeatedly praised editing and rhythm** ("best editing", "sweeping editing", "sense of rhythm"). Several teams credit human editors, colourists, composers and SFX artists - AI generation plus a real post-production pipeline.
4. **Human audio** was allowed here (one HM did all sound design by hand; another used Suno for a song). This is **not** allowed in the Global Film Festival, where all audio must be AI-generated.
5. **Tool stacks named by winners:** Nano Banana / Nano Banana Pro and Soul 2.0 for stills and characters, Cinema Studio (2.0) for shots, the Angles 2.0 app for coverage, Kling 3.0 and Seedance 2.0 for motion (one author reports 3,000+ credits for an action-heavy short with Seedance 2.0).
6. **Engagement counted for 30%.** Top gallery views ranged from ~290k (an HM) to ~9k; the winner had ~175k views and ~1.6k likes. Posting on several platforms (Instagram, X, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn) was common among winners.
7. Several winners published their entry as a **project folder**; the 1st-place, Kaliyug Rising and PLAYGROUND RULES projects allow **Clone project**, which is the fastest way to study how the shots were built.

No generation prompts are published on any submission page; the only way to see them is to clone a project where cloning is enabled.
