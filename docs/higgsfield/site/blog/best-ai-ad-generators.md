# 9 Best AI Ad Generators in 2026 (Tested & Compared)

Higgsfield, Jul 28, 2026 (listicle)  
Prompts extracted: 0

Choose by: ad format, starting point (URL/images vs script/avatar/footage), pricing unit, consistency needs.
**Higgsfield Marketing Studio** (this article's version): product URL -> imports images, name, features, pricing -> script; **nine ad formats** (UGC, product review, TV spot, high-motion…); **≤15 s**; one generation can combine product images, spokesperson reference, voiceover recording and camera-style reference; native audio + lip sync. Note: **"Marketing Studio does not automatically carry a person's identity from one generation to the next" — attach the same spokesperson reference image to every generation** (other articles describe attaching a Soul ID). One output per generation; no standalone images here. Pro $29/mo ≈ $2.90 per 10 s 720p.
Others: Creatify (URL -> avatar video, $1.65/15 s on Pro; set inputs before spending credits), Arcads (1,000+ actors, product placed in actor's hand, free hook generator, $5.20/15 s), HeyGen (Avatar V ~$5.80/15 s), Runway (Director Mode camera separate from action, Motion Brush; ~$2.02/10 s), Synthesia ($2.90/min), Pika (effects, ~$1/10 s), Predis.ai (posts + scheduling), OpusClip (repurposing, ~$0.10/min source).
Common combo: URL tool (Higgsfield/Creatify) for the core video + Pika for an effect hook + a scheduler.
