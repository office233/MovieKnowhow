## Lesson 4 — With MCP vs. Claude Alone

URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/build-3d-games-mcp/mcp-vs-claude-alone

- Same one-sentence pirate prompt run twice: Claude + MCP + skill vs Claude alone. Same working logic (sailing, cannons, boarding); Claude-alone = no skins/textures/ocean, grey shapes.
- Bigger gap: **multiplayer hosting**. Normally a backend developer, weeks of player sync, monthly servers. Here a friend just clicks a link - the MCP hosts the match and syncs players automatically.

## Lesson 5 — Scene 2: Blockfield Shooter

URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/build-3d-games-mcp/scene-2-blockfield-shooter

- Block-world shooter, two teams, place/destroy blocks.
- The skill **interviews you first** (like a studio scoping a client), then writes a **design document** (mechanics, art direction, level structure, sound) - the doc, not the one-liner, builds the game.
- It then **deploys automatically** to a live link; with a plain chatbot you'd be the middleman handling infrastructure.
- Weapons differ: sniper slow + zoom + heavy damage; bazooka rockets with smoke trail that destroy blocks.

### Verbatim prompts (1)

_Target / settings for this lesson:_ Claude (Fable 5) + Higgsfield MCP + game skill

**Prompt #1 — video cue 0:22, generation prompt**

```text
Build a block-world shooter with two teams against each other, where I can place and destroy blocks and fight the opposite team.
```

## Lesson 6 — Scene 3: NeonSlice Fruit Slicer

URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/build-3d-games-mcp/scene-3-neonslice-fruit-slicer

- Fruit slicer controlled by the **webcam**: your fingertip is the blade. Losing hand tracking costs a blade; hitting a bomb costs a heart.
- Fable + MCP combined hand tracking, velocity prediction and physics into one browser game from one prompt; no install, no extra hardware.

## Lesson 7 — Publishing to the Marketplace

URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/build-3d-games-mcp/publishing-to-the-marketplace

- After building, the skill asks whether to **publish to the Higgsfield marketplace**. Deployed = only people you send the link to; published = discoverable, playable and **remixable** by strangers.
- Timing argument: marketplace launched that week and was nearly empty - be first (like early App Store flashlight apps in 2008). Every play is free signal about what's fun.

## Lesson 9 — What Three Games Actually Cost

URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/build-3d-games-mcp/what-three-games-cost
