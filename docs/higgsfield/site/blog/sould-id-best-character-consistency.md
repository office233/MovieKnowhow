## Notes

**Topic:** Soul ID explained (Jun 29 2026). No prompts.
- Train a digital double from your photos (or anyone's with usage rights): **20+ recommended (up to 80)**, **960 px+**, training **3-5 min**, **25 credits (~$1.25)**, runs in Soul 2.0 on paid plans; 20+ (elsewhere "50+") style presets (e.g., Amalfi Summer, Gorpcore Outdoor, 0.5 Selfie, editorial, retro, Y2K).
- Why it beats reference matching: references anchor one image/angle/light and drift when the scene changes; Soul ID learns the face as a whole — holds across lighting, pose, scenes, sessions, and models (images and video: Seedance 2.0, Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0, WAN 2.6).
- Steps: upload clear varied photos -> train -> pick style presets -> generate images/videos -> reuse at scale.
- Tips: avoid sunglasses, masks, extreme expressions; consistent lighting across training photos; pick brand-appropriate presets early and don't switch often (hurts audience recognition); use the same avatar across platforms/formats; refresh the avatar if the brand evolves while keeping the core identity.
- Pair with Cinema Studio: Soul ID locks *who*, Cinema Studio locks *how the camera moves* (dolly, orbit, tracking) — e.g., same spokesperson in a Seedance commercial dolly, a Kling tracking sequence, a Veo close-up.
