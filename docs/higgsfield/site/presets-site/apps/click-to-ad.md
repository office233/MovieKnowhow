## What it does

Generate marketing videos, storyboards, and images with Higgsfield AI.

**Click to Ad: an entry point to Marketing Studio.** Paste a product link (or use an Ad Reference) and get UGC and professional video ads. Marketing Studio sections: UGC, Product shot, Motion, Ads, Posters, Marketplace. Tools: **Ad Reference** and **Product Link**, with projects to organize work. Templates visible on the crawled page: Ice Cube Hover, Brushed Tin, Chilled Can, Emerald Pajamas, Linen Lid Lift, Golden Dew, Sky High Chili, Blue Sky Cap (product shots); Green Tea Macro, Fisheye Jelly Balloon, Monospace Callouts (product motion); How-To (UGC video). See `docs/higgsfield/presets/marketing-studio/` for full template data.
