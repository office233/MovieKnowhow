# How to Turn Your Own Footage Into Cinematic VFX with Seedance 4K: Guide with Ready-To-Use Prompts

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/blog/VFX-with-Seedance-4k  
Mariam Barova, Jun 26, 2026  
Prompts extracted: 6

**Video-to-video VFX workflow with Seedance (4K)** — keep your real performance and camera, swap the world.
1. **Shoot raw footage** — no green screen or markers needed; natural performance, clear light, stable reference. **Shoot the camera move you want in the final shot** (e.g. pull back and rush past the "flying" actor) — Seedance keeps the motion from your footage.
2. **Let Claude analyze the clip**: identity, exact motion/performance, camera move and framing, lens, lighting, timing, aspect ratio + fps -> backbone of the prompt.
3. **Describe your idea to Claude**; it writes a prompt that locks subject, camera and performance, then layers only the new world/creature.
4. **Paste prompt into Seedance 4K over the uploaded footage** (`<<<video_1>>>`) and generate native 4K.
Prompt patterns that recur (reuse them):
- **SOURCE LOCK** block: keep 1:1 identity, face, exact performance/timing, camera (path, angle, framing, lens, speed, shake) and the source edit/cuts; "Do NOT re-frame/re-time/re-angle".
- Allow controlled changes only: wardrobe reskin, posture tweak (standing -> seated riding), environment, added creatures.
- **HARD LIGHT/COLOR MATCH** of added elements to the plate (same grade, haze, aerial perspective, contact shadows, grain; "never pasted").
- **Anti-slop realism** for creatures (wet wrinkled hide, living eyes, mist partly hiding them, telephoto look, slow heavy motion) and **proportion locks** (small head vs large body, no morphing across the cut).
- Timed beats (threat beat at 0:02–0:03 on the actor's turn; creatures appear ~1 s in).
- **Audio: discard all original audio; generate new diegetic SFX**, no music.
- FORBIDDEN list + duration + ratio "match <<<video_1>>>".
