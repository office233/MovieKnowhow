# Effect preset hubs: Viral, UGC, Commercial

Sources: https://higgsfield.ai/viral (50 presets + 6 "Mixed" combos), https://higgsfield.ai/ugc (45), https://higgsfield.ai/commercial (27). One-click image-to-video presets; each preset is bound to a model family (`preset_family`): Kling, MiniMax (Hailuo), Seedance (Pro), or Higgsfield DOP. Workflow for all: upload one photo (person or product) -> pick preset -> generate; no prompt needed (optional prompt refines).

## How to choose
- **Viral** = shock/VFX transformations for social hooks (Earth Zoom Out, Face Punch, Building Explosion, Turning Metal, Melting, Disintegration, Angel Wings, Tattoo Animation, Raven Transition, Seamless Transition). "Mixed" presets chain two effects (e.g. "Turning Metal + Melting", "Turning Metal + Eyes In", "Thunder God + Turning Metal").
- **UGC** = creator-style clips with a real-feeling person (Selfie, Selfie Outfit, Outfit Check, Morning routine, Eating, Crying, Shocked, Happy, Hair Style, Fix and pose, Group Photo) plus light VFX (Money Rain, Clothes Rain, Explosion, Giant Grab).
- **Commercial** = product/brand showcase moves (3D Rotation, Arc Left/Right, Robo Arm, Exploded View, Bullet Time, Paint Splash, Freezing, Cotton Cloud, Catwalk, Red Carpet, Yacht, Beach Ride, Car Chasing, Road Rush, Timelapse Glam, Roll Transition).
- "Top Choice" tags mark the platform's most-used presets.
