## Notes

**Title on page:** "How to Built a Full Clothing Brand with AI — Full Prompts" (Mariam Barova, Apr 26 2026). Brand "HIGGS" (acid yellow #d1fe17 + black).

**Step 1 — brand:** logos should be obvious and simple. Have **Claude write the image prompt**, then generate 4 options in Soul Cinema (4 images ≈ 0.5 credit — experiment freely). Refine the chosen logo in Nano Banana Pro ("recreate exactly ... change only the wordmark"), then a mini brand sheet: monogram, standalone symbol, third variation, each on white and black (6 outputs).
**Step 2 — product line** (t-shirt, puffer jacket, trail sneakers, parachute pants, shield sunglasses): ghost-mannequin/flat-lay product mockups on #FFFFFF with structured spec prompts (FABRIC & TEXTURE, SILHOUETTE, BASE COLOR, PANEL CONSTRUCTION, LOGO placement/size, Technical line). Tip: design with a placeholder word, then **swap in the real logo with Nano Banana Pro**. Sneakers as a 2x2 four-view technical sheet. Pants bridge the loud jacket and minimal tee (black with one lime stripe).
**Step 3 — ads in Marketing Studio** (install the Claude Skill from the video description; give it avatar + product images and it writes Marketing Studio prompts):
- UGC 1 **no prompt**: paste the product website link -> Marketing Studio scrapes all items and dresses the avatar in the full collection. A UGC video costs < $5 (vs $200-500 IRL).
- UGC 2 Pro Virtual Try-On: static camera, glasses thrown at lens then boomerang back onto face, **liquid mercury scan transition** swaps location and outfit (timed 0-3/4-7/8-10/11-15 s, 9:16).
- UGC 3 UGC Try-On: 5 short clips, same static full-body framing, one item added per clip; state "NO bag... hands empty unless specified".
- UGC 4 skateboard outfit change: one side-on Cinemascope take; items float at body height and appear on him as he skates through ("no animation, no effect, no flash").
- Hyper Motion (CGI/Apple-style): logo "with comet tail" (tiny prompt); jacket element-by-element assembly (**no prompt, no avatar**); "stress test of this jacket with natural elements".
- Wild Card: levitation through clouds (one handheld take, wire-work practical VFX language); **vertical "cuts in space" between six locations** in one Cinemascope take.
- TV Spot: monochrome city, only the clothing in colour, fast negative-invert flashes (2-3 frames).
- Bonus: packaging concept (bag + box) in Nano Banana Pro from the logo; **Unboxing** preset with the packaging image as an additional asset, no prompt.
