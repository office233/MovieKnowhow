# Netflix’s Moria Hybrid Campaign by Vertex CGI with Higgsfield

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/case-studies/vertexcgi-moria-campaign-higgsfield
Published: Sep 11, 2026 (5 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: customer story (Vertex CGI x Netflix Latin America, series "Moria" about Moria Casan). 360 campaign mixing physical giant installations (shoe, lipstick, wig) with 3 AI "installations" (oversized nails, rooftop intervention, Planetarium). Tools: Nano Banana Pro, Kling 3.0, Supercomputer. Result: one Instagram post 1.7M views plus earned media and reposts.

**Synthetic-OOH (fake out-of-home) recipe**
1. Start from real location references and coordinates.
2. Match live conditions: weather, what people are wearing, time of day, so the fake fits the real city that day.
3. Generate the scene in Nano Banana Pro using existing campaign assets (key art of the star) as references.
4. Composite key visuals/logos in Photoshop where needed, then pass the composite back through Nano Banana Pro to blend it into the scene and raise realism (keeps logos and key visuals accurate).
5. Lock the still, then animate with Kling.
6. Release alongside real installations so real and synthetic layers read as one experience (real in city, hyperreal on social).
