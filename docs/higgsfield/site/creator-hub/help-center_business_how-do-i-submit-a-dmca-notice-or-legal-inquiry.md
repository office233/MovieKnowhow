# How do I submit a DMCA notice or legal inquiry?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/business/how-do-i-submit-a-dmca-notice-or-legal-inquiry
Published: Aug 2, 2026 (3 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (legal). DMCA notices to support@higgsfield.ai with the six required elements (signature, copyrighted work, infringing material location, contact info, good-faith statement, accuracy/authority statement under penalty of perjury); acknowledgment within 48 h; counter-notification possible. Report IP/likeness violations or wrongly flagged original content (give time, model, prompt; blocked gens have no ID). Verified rights holders can request recurring access for authorized IP. Non-consensual intimate images removed within 48 h of a valid report. Compliance docs (SOC 2, DPA, W-9, etc.) only with Enterprise; trust.higgsfield.ai.
