Third Skin Enhancer article (see also The-Skin-Enhancer-How-to-Retouch-Pictures-with-AI.md). Explains face detail restoration (reconstructs pixels from learned face geometry instead of sharpening), texture-preserving smoothing, and semantic understanding of skin/light/expression.
Fixes: blurry faces (no halos), noise without plastic look, tone/color shifts from cheap sensors, eyes/brows/lashes, social-media compression damage, old scans.
Practical tips:
1. Use the highest-resolution source available.
2. Avoid pre-filtered/edited inputs.
3. Keep the face unobstructed (sunglasses, hair, extreme shadow reduce accuracy).
4. Apply at **70–85% intensity** for a professional balance.
5. For group photos, **enhance each face as an individual crop**.
