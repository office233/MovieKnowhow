# How Sinecera, the Studio Behind Films for Lacoste, SuperStep and Nesine, Runs Hybrid Production on Higgsfield

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/case-studies/sinecera-hybrid-production-higgsfield
Published: Aug 4, 2026 (10 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: customer story (Sinecera, Istanbul production studio: commercials, 2D animation, features, games; 20 years conventional experience). Tools: Cinema Studio, AI Cast, Soul ID, AI Locations, Cinematic Cameras, Multishot.

**Numbers**: a campaign film needing 3-4 shoot days now needs 1 or 0; full-AI core team 5-6 people vs 30-50 on location; a revision changes from "reshoot" to "regenerate". Animated feature "Cha Cha Bird" (70 min) made feasible.

**Pipeline**: script -> boards -> previz -> offline -> online, with a client approval gate at every step. Higgsfield covers boards through much of final imagery.

**Techniques**
- One dedicated Cinema Studio project per job; the whole team (5-6 people) generates into that shared project: same cast, same locations, same look.
- AI Cast + Soul ID hold a recurring face across a whole commercial; AI Locations hold a consistent world; Cinematic Cameras give real camera language.
- Rule: always animate from an approved keyframe (an art-directed, signed-off still). Text-to-video is the exception.
- Multishot turns approved storyboard frames into moving shots across different models without losing the approved frame.
- Hybrid example, Nesine "100th Gazi Kosusu" (Turkish Derby since 1927): real spectators shot on green screen for emotion; hippodromes, crowds, eras and a century of aging world generated behind them.
- Full-AI example: Lacoste x SuperStep fashion film built entirely in one Cinema Studio project.
- "Create with truth": deliberately keep human flaws/mess in frames; choosing which flaws to keep is a human job.
- Sell the process (approval gates, honest about seams), not the technology; clients now ask "can you deliver Thursday?".
