Patterns in the top-liked 4K prompts:
- **One continuous take is stated three ways** ("one single continuous unbroken shot", "no cuts, no edits, no transitions", "single continuous take") - redundancy is deliberate to stop the model cutting.
- **Time-coded pacing inside a 10-15 s clip** ("about 4-5 seconds of ... then ... at the midpoint ... the rest in ...").
- Camera language is explicit: chase from behind, drive in, bullet-time orbit, macro, with motion blur resolving into frozen detail.
- Texture and resolution words are packed at the end (ultra-detailed textures, HDR, 4K, documentary look).
- Nearly all are text-to-video with no image input (`images: []`), 16:9, 10-15 s.
