## Notes

**Topic:** Higgsfield Audio launch (David Matamoros, Mar 2 2026). No prompts.
- Three tools in the Audio tab (opens in a Cinema Studio 2-style window; switch at bottom-left):
  - **Voiceover (TTS):** text -> audio; models Eleven v3, MiniMax Speech 2.8 HD, VibeVoice; 40+ preset voices or up to **3 custom voices**; 70+ input languages.
  - **Change Voice:** replace the voice in an uploaded video with a preset (21) or custom voice (e.g., swap to presets "Roman" or "Vesper" for a gritty character).
  - **Translate:** translate a video's speech with **lip sync**; at launch English, Mandarin, French, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Turkish (Spanish, Arabic, German announced).
- **Custom voice:** Voiceover or Change Voice -> Voice Preset -> Add Voice -> upload MP3/WAV or record (read the sample text, clearly, up to 2 min) -> Clone voice.
- Tips: clean, noise-free, high-quality recordings; high-quality video input; for Translate keep the speaker's face clearly visible throughout (better lip sync).
- Use cases: localise hit videos; faceless narration; character dubbing for AI films; e-learning/corporate training translated for branches.
