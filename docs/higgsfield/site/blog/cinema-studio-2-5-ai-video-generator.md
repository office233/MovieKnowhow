## Notes

**What it is:** Cinema Studio 2.5 (Mar 2026) = Cinema Studio 2 + built-in **Soul Cast** AI actors at the start of the workflow + an in-studio **color grade** step at the end.

**Soul Cast (AI actor builder)** — you define: Genre, Budget of the actor, Era, Archetype, Identity, Physical appearance (height, eye colour, hair, facial hair...), Details, Outfit. Selling point: realistic skin texture / emotion, avoids the waxy "plastic" AI look, and follows the chosen era's style.

**Workflow (character-and-location-first):**
1. Add up to **3 Soul Cast characters** to the key frame (from saved Elements or generate on the spot). Tip: click **"Save to Elements"** so you can re-summon the same actor later.
2. Prompt the **location** — specific prompts work best, but even a short one gets a cinematic look.
3. Generate the key frame (hero frame). Cinema Studio's philosophy: perfect the starting image, because the animation inherits its quality.
4. Click the key frame -> **Colorgrade**: colour temperature (warm/cool), contrast + saturation, sharpness + effects, highlights, film grain, exposure.
5. Animate the graded key frame into video.

**Grade recipes mentioned:** summer campaign = warm, high contrast, slightly overexposed; premium editorial = cool, desaturated, film grain; pop/R&B music video = bloom + warmth; electronic/hip-hop = hard contrast + cool temperature.

**Use cases:** narrative shorts (same character across locations + one unified grade across all clips), brand content matching visual guidelines, music videos, creator persona content, previs.
