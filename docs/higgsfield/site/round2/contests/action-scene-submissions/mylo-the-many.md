## Description (author's own words)

> Mylo used to be a starship; now, they’re a gardener. In this society, "rehabilitation" for a misbehaving synthetic means being stuffed into the humming, four-inch chassis of a Pollinator droid. But Mylo has lived too many lives to play by the rules, and a starship-sized ego doesn't downsize easily. Between pollinating space-wheat and sparring with a skeptical parole officer named Carol, Mylo is finding that "giving back" to the galaxy is a lot harder when you still believe you're a god.
