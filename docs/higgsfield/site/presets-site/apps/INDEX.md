# Higgsfield Apps (one-click presets): index

Apps are **prompt-free** one-click presets: upload a photo, product image or clip, maybe pick an option, and click Generate. None of the 101 app pages exposes a text prompt, so [PROMPTS.md](PROMPTS.md) has no verbatim prompts and instead gives an input recipe per use-case. Costs are credits from the page data (template cost) plus the number printed on the Generate button, which sometimes differs. Most trend/ad apps have a 1-2 credit image mode and a 15-26 credit video mode.
