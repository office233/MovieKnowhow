# How to Make Product Videos for TikTok and Reels Without Filming: 10 AI Formats That Work

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/blog/Product-Videos-TikTok-Reels-Without-Filming  
Higgsfield (2026)  
Prompts extracted: 1

Match each product to a proven short-form format and generate with a vertical preset (presets now run on **Seedance 2.0**; they pre-configure pacing, framing and 9:16 export). Product image -> clip in under 10 minutes.

| # | Format | Preset | Tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unboxing Surprise | Unpacking | add ambient audio (tearing paper, soft gasp); 9:16 |
| 2 | Before & After | Luxury Ad or Minimalist Corporate | feed before + after frames; transition 2–3 s |
| 3 | Day in the Life (Hyperlite) | Dynamic Sport Ad | name the light ("Morning glow through window") |
| 4 | POV Walkthrough | First-Person POV with Product | write directional flow: where subject walks, what they approach, how the frame ends |
| 5 | Reaction Cut | Reaction | end on secondary angle or zoom-out for re-watches |
| 6 | Trend Remix | Gen-Z TikTok Edit | match tone/rhythm but rewrite the visual story |
| 7 | ASMR Product Focus | ASMR | minimal audio; near-silence raises perceived quality |
| 8 | Streamer/Vlog Highlight | Streamer Highlight | add captions (mute viewing) |
| 9 | Epic Fail to Hero | Epic Fail | short fail, fast cut to payoff |
| 10 | Minimal Product Loop | Minimalist Corporate | loops < 7 s; gentle rotation for depth; entry/exit frames aligned |

Limits: best for scripted/product content (real-footage reactions -> CapCut/DaVinci); clips cap ~15 s — stitch longer videos in Cinema Studio with same preset + Soul ID; entry plan ($15) has **Seedance 2.0 Fast** only (full Seedance 2.0 on Plus/Pro); vague prompts/low-res images -> generic output; presets feel templated if overused.
Daily pipeline: one format -> Marketing Studio template -> product image + Soul ID -> render 3–4 short variations (1080p clip ≈ 40 s on Supercomputer) -> fix flicker/exposure, Upscale (or Topaz) to 1080p/4K -> export 9:16 (also 1:1, 16:9).
Hook in the first 2 seconds. Sora app closed Apr 26, 2026; Sora API retires Sep 24, 2026 — former "Sora 2 Trends" presets now run on Seedance 2.0 (Kling 3.0 alternative for 4K/consistent voices). Master one format before adding more.
