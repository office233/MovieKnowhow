# Mixed Media intro / landing page

- URL: https://higgsfield.ai/mixed-media-intro
- Positioned as an "After Effects alternative for viral edits". It turns any video into mixed-media art: 40+ curated looks (Neon, Doodle, Grime, Sketch, paper, comic and more), makes complete 10-second scenes, and claims to be "100x faster than manual editing".

## How it works (in my own words)

1. Upload a source video. The feature is video-to-video; the landing copy about "text prompts" and "mood boards" is placeholder text reused on every step.
2. Choose a preset (see [INDEX.md](INDEX.md)).
3. Choose frame rate and resolution. Per the preset data: 4-24 fps; 1k, 2k or 4k; clip 1-10 s.
4. Optional advanced settings: preset-specific colour pickers (main object, background, outline, fragment colours) and instrument selectors (marker, spray, gouache, crayon, pencil, watercolor, brush, oil; line style curved or straight; top-layer doodles such as Japanese calligraphy, childish or random).
5. Generate. The AI masks the subject frame by frame (automatic rotoscoping) and stylises the subject and background layers separately.

## Practical tips

- Use a clean, well-lit clip with one clear subject. The masking separates subject from background, and presets like Noir, Two Color and Ultraviolet recolour the two layers differently.
- Keep clips short (up to 10 s), because cost scales with frames. Try 8 fps at 1k first (about 16 credits per second), then re-run the one you like at 2k or 4k with higher fps.
- Use it as a stylised music-video, fashion-edit or social-hook layer on top of footage you already have, for example a Higgsfield-generated clip.
