## Lesson 2 — Inventing the Brand

URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/brand-visuals-ai/inventing-the-brand

- Order: brand before product before ads.
- Logo brief goes to Claude first (brand name HIGGS, accent **#d1fe17**, flat vector, minimal). Tell Claude to aim for *memorable, not clever* (think three stripes / swoosh / apple).
- Generate in **Soul Cinema**: 4 options for **half a credit** -> experiment freely.
- Only flaw: wordmark too small -> ask Claude for a follow-up edit prompt (bolder, slightly tilted text) and run it in **Nano Banana Pro** with the logo attached as reference.
- Keep a **Notion checklist** of everything the brand needs. Choose colours deliberately (emotional/category signals), not by taste; the demo borrows Higgsfield's brand colours.
- Variations: Claude prompt -> Nano Banana Pro + logo -> monogram, icon, horizontal lockup, each in 2 colourways.

## Lesson 4 — Marketing Studio Try-Ons

URL: https://higgsfield.ai/academy/courses/brand-visuals-ai/marketing-studio-try-ons

- **Marketing Studio** presets map to 2026 ad formats (Hyper Motion, Unboxing, UGC, TV Spot, try-ons, Wildcard...). Point it at a **product URL** and it scrapes the product details.
- **Zero-prompt UGC**: product pasted from the brand site + a custom named avatar ("Lulu") -> Generate with no prompt. A UGC video that costs **$200-500** to produce comes out for a few dollars; the avatar wore the whole collection with consistent logos.
- For control: a custom Claude skill writes Marketing Studio prompts from product + avatar images.
- **Pro Virtual Try-On** (transformation ad): on an overpass she throws sunglasses at camera; as they land on her face a liquid-scan wave turns the overpass into a dark parking garage and the outfit materialises. ~2 min generation; logo holds through the transition.
- **UGC Try-On**: "a viral UGC video with my avatar and this product" -> 5 beats (tee, jacket, pants+sneakers, sunglasses, full look to camera); a background reflection adds candid realism.
- Single-product Pro Virtual Try-On (sunglasses) ends on a pack shot with built-in music - postable as-is.
