## Soul 2.0
- Higgsfield's own foundation photo model for fashion-aware, culture-native, editorial aesthetics; understands fashion terms, slang and cultural references; spontaneous poses and angles.
- Text or image as the starting point; 20+ curated presets at launch (Mystique city, Warm ambient, Editorial street style, Subtle flash, Old smartphone, Frutiger Aero, Swag era, Y2K outside, Nature light, Y2K Studio, Theatrical light, Siren...). Community data also shows "Flash editorial", "General", "Digital camera", "Surreal solarization", "Y2K street", "Candy pop", "Street photography".
- **Soul ID**: train a character from your photos once; identity stays locked across styles, poses, lighting - base for AI influencers, campaigns, personal branding.
- **Soul HEX**: upload a reference image and pull its colour palette into your style.
- **Soul Moodboard**: turn references into a moodboard that defines style/tone.
- **Soul Cinema**: cinematic film-grade image variant (used for film stills/locations).
- Powers avatars in Marketing Studio; API: Soul 2 $0.0032/image, Soul Standard $0.0938/image. Free generation pools for Soul 2.0/Soul Cinema come with some plans (see pricing.md).

## Soul v1 preset parameters (from embedded job data)
Typical settings on the showcased presets: 1344x2016 (2:3) or 2048x1536 / 1536x2048 at "1080p" quality, steps 50, batch 2-4, style strength 1, enhance_prompt sometimes off. Soul community (Soul 2.0) jobs: 1536x2048 (3:4) mostly, quality 1080p, enhance off, presets mostly "General" or "Flash editorial".

## How the best Soul prompts are written (patterns)
- Declare the **capture device and its artefacts**: "candid iPhone snapshot", "early-2000s digital camera with harsh direct flash, visible noise, slight over-sharpening", "CCD camera grain".
- Describe **framing imperfection** on purpose: "slight tilt", "off-centre", "informal crop", "fleeting handheld capture".
- Wardrobe with **brand/material specificity** (COS silk blouse, Our Legacy leather pants, Arc'teryx windbreaker, chainmail) and texture words (pores, freckles, fabric grain, condensation).
- Lighting source named (direct flash, window light, wall sconce, golden hour) + palette.
- Soul 2.0 community prompts often end with a **HEX VALUES: [...]** palette list (Soul HEX) - a way to lock colour grade.
- Keep the style preset aligned with the prompt (Y2K preset + Y2K description; Gorpcore preset + outdoor tech wear).
