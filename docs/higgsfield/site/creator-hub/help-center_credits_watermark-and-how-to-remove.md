# Why is there a watermark, and how do I remove it?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/credits/watermark-and-how-to-remove
Published: Aug 3, 2026 (2 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article. Visible watermark only on free accounts; any paid plan (credits, free gens, Unlimited) is watermark-free. No toggle; existing free-account outputs must be regenerated after upgrading. Invisible machine-readable AI markers/metadata may be embedded regardless of plan. Old files are unchanged after cancellation.
