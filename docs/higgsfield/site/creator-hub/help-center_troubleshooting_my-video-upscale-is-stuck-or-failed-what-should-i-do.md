# My video upscale is stuck or failed. What should I do?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/troubleshooting/my-video-upscale-is-stuck-or-failed-what-should-i-do
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (3 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (troubleshooting + upscale model guide). Upscales normally take 1-5 min; if >5 min cancel from the tile and retry.
- Video upscalers: **ByteDance Upscale** (high fidelity, up to 8K, preset-tuned) vs **Topaz Video** (diffusion-based, invents creative detail).
- Image upscalers: **Topaz** (default, general), **ByteDance Upscale** (up to 4K), **Topaz Generative** (diffusion, adds detail).
- Rule: ByteDance/default Topaz sharpen what exists; diffusion options hallucinate fine detail: use them for creative work, avoid for product shots, text-heavy visuals and already-approved client deliverables.
- Scale factor selector + Advanced settings; cost on Upscale button. Failures: use MP4/H.264, avoid tiny/over-compressed sources, queue overload. Credits auto-refunded.
