# Higgsfield Affiliate Program

Sources:
- https://higgsfield.ai/affiliate

Blog summary already exists: [../../blog/higgsfield-affiliate-program-2026.md](../../blog/higgsfield-affiliate-program-2026.md).

- Earn **up to 25%** of what referrals pay, for **up to 12 months** per referral (monthly plans pay each renewal; annual/quarterly plans are credited up front). All subscription purchases, renewals and upgrades in the first 12 months count. Paid in **cash**, not credits. Rate rises with the revenue you generate.
- Referrals get **up to 50% off for 3 hours** after logging in through your link.
- Apply with a short form; link is live immediately. Established creators: affiliate@higgsfield.ai.
- Not allowed: paid ads to the link, bidding on the Higgsfield brand, coupon/cashback sites. Organic promotion only.
- The earnings calculator's example (160 new referrals/month at $200/month average spend) shows cumulative commission rising from $8,000 in month 1 to $624,000 over 12 months - an illustration at the 25% maximum rate.
