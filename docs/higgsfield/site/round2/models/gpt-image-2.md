# GPT Image 2 landing (/gpt-1.5)

Sources:
- https://higgsfield.ai/gpt-1.5

The /gpt-1.5 URL now serves the **GPT Image 2** landing (canonical /gpt-2), already covered in [../../features/models/gpt-image-2.md](../../features/models/gpt-image-2.md).

Claims worth keeping: native 4K photoreal output with the old warm "AI cast" gone; **over 95% text accuracy** including Chinese, Japanese and Korean on curved surfaces, small sizes and dense layouts; lock a character, product or brand asset across storyboards and campaign variants. Three steps: optional reference image -> prompt (signage, packaging, multilingual text, complex compositions) -> generate and download. The "Random prompt" button's prompts are loaded client-side and were not in the HTML.
