# What are Exclusive Offers?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/plans/what-are-exclusive-offers
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (3 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (plans). Personalized limited-time offers for active subscribers: Upgrade offer (higher tier for one cycle at promo price, then renews at that tier's standard price) or Add-on offer (bonus credits, temporary Unlimited on specific models, priority processing). Countdown purchase window; cannot be reactivated after expiry/cancel/refund; not available on request.
