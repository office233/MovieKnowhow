## Notes

**Topic:** Higgsfield Global Film Festival rules (Aug 10 2026). No prompts.
- $1,000,000 pool: 1st $500k, 2nd $200k, 3rd $100k, Audience Choice $100k, 10 Honorable Mentions x $10k; one prize per participant/team.
- Eligibility: 18+, Higgsfield account, solo or team of up to 4 (Team Leader owns project/prize). Unlimited separate entries.
- Film rules: any genre; **min 3 min** (recommended max 5); 16:9 or 21:9; MP4/MOV up to 4K; any language with English subtitles or VO; all video/image generated on higgsfield.ai (any model) **from scratch inside the Generation Window** (earlier generations allowed as references); **no real person's face or voice as input, including your own**; music/voice/SFX must be AI-generated and uploaded into the submission project with rights.
- Submission: final video with Higgsfield watermark + packshot, plus a public post (Instagram, YouTube, X or Reddit) viewable without login. Deadline Sep 15 2026 23:59 UTC; winners late Oct 2026.
- Built in **Cinema Studio** (submission project lives there from first generation to final cut).
- Judging criteria: directing & storytelling, visual craft, sound & editing, originality & concept. Jury: Edwin Catmull, Phedon Papamichael, Paul W.S. Anderson.
- **Open-source reference projects** worth studying (every prompt, asset, generation public): *Hell Grind*, *Zephyr* (see guide-youtube-seedance2.0.md), *Cully Hill Boys*.
