**Kling (2.6) Motion Control** = Reference Image (your character) + Motion Reference ("driving") Video -> the character performs the video's movement, expression and pacing while keeping identity. Offered "unlimited" on some plans at the time.

Strengths: complex athletics/dance/martial arts with weight transfer and momentum; improved hand/finger articulation; text prompt can change the environment/add elements while the motion continues (example prompt: "A corgi runs in, circling around a girl's feet"); orientation modes choose whether to follow the reference video's camera or the source image's framing.

Source image rules:
- All limbs visible. Hands hidden in pockets while the motion waves -> hallucinated hands, 6-finger glitches, blur.
- Leave negative space around the subject so wide arm moves/dancing don't clip the frame.

Driving video rules:
- Clear subject, clean background, high-contrast distinct silhouette.
- **Match framing**: close-up talking reference for a close-up image; a full-body walk driving a portrait image causes shaking/warped faces.

Use-cases: virtual influencers/brand mascots driven by your team's own performances; hand-with-product demos; **localization — one hero motion video re-used with different character images (ethnicities, ages) at zero filming cost**.
