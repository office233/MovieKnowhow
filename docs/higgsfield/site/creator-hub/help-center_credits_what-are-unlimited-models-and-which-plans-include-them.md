# What are Unlimited models, and which plans include them?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/credits/what-are-unlimited-models-and-which-plans-include-them
Published: Aug 3, 2026 (4 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (credits). Unlimited = specific models generate without credits for a set period.
- Included on mid/top plans (list and period on each Pricing card, "Unlimited & Free Gens"); most 365 days, newest flagships shorter (e.g. 7 days); some models get free-gen pools instead.
- Turn on with the **Unlimited toggle** in the generation panel; off = Credit Mode (priority queue, max speed). Unlimited runs in the standard queue (slower at peak). Active list: Manage Account -> Subscription -> Active unlimited models.
- 365 promo: one-time, only works while a subscription is active; paused if subscription lapses; lost if you downgrade to a plan without it; does not reset at renewal.
- Unlimited Models Marketplace bundles per model and resolution tier (top tier includes lower); concurrency: video 1 at a time up to 15 s, image 2 at a time, All Models 2 image + 1 video.
- Fair use: personal human use only; no automation/scripting/credential sharing/reselling (may be throttled or paused). Never applies via MCP, CLI, Canvas, Supercomputer.
