# How do I submit a GDPR, CCPA, or privacy request?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/account/how-do-i-submit-a-gdpr-ccpa-or-privacy-request
Published: Aug 2, 2026 (2 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (account/legal). Deletion = self-service. Data export for GDPR/UK DPA users via privacy@higgsfield.ai (response up to 30 days GDPR, 45 days CCPA). CCPA/other requests: privacy@higgsfield.ai, subject "Privacy Request". Opt out of training going forward by deleting content/account; Enterprise content never used for training. DMCA/legal: support@higgsfield.ai.
