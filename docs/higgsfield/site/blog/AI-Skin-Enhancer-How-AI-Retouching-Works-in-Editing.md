Explainer of AI skin enhancement tech (companion to the Skin Enhancer how-to).
- Pipeline concepts: segmentation (skin vs hair/brows/background/clothes, so only skin is smoothed), texture-preserving diffusion (re-creates pores), detail-aware face restoration (symmetry, light direction, expression), lighting reconstruction (highlights/shadows behave like real skin).
- Replaces manual healing, frequency separation, dodge & burn; benefits: consistency, speed, scalability.
- Relevant for AI filmmaking: **post-enhance AI-generated portraits** — fixes blurry cheeks, distorted pores, harsh shadows, overexposed highlights; "prepping images for video conversion" (clean the start frame before image-to-video). Also works on video skin.
- Higgsfield's version: preserves pores/micro-shadows/contours, high-res output, tone balancing, one-click.
