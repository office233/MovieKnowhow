# Make AI Videos From Text and URLs With Higgsfield Explainer

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/blog/ai-video-from-text-and-url  
Higgsfield, Jul 14, 2026  
Prompts extracted: 1

**Explainer** = separate tool that turns a topic, file or URL into a complete narrated, structured video (20 s – 10 min) in one consistent style.
Flow:
1. **Preset** (10+): Pixel Art (8/16-bit RPG look — gaming, tech, history), Claymotion (stop-motion clay — kids, character stories, bedtime), Mixed Media (photo + illustration collage — brand/product walkthroughs), 3D Papercraft (folded paper, soft shadows — process/finance/edu), 2D Illustrator (flat bold motion-graphics — brand/social), Whiteboard Doodle (concept explanations with diagrams), more.
2. **Input** ("What should the video explain?"): typed topic, attached files, or a URL via Link (reads the page: product page, article, docs).
3. **Format**: aspect ratio (16:9 default; vertical/square), duration (20 s, 30 s, 1, 3, 5, 10 min, Manual), voice (40+ presets or custom voice).
4. Generate — narration synced in the same pass; output is one finished video, not clips.
Cost: ~72 credits (~$3.60) for 20 s; scales with duration — 3 min+ typically > $20. Match duration to how much the story needs.
Recommended pairings: bedtime story = Claymotion, 1–3 min, warm voice (e.g. "a rabbit who wants to reach the moon"); brand explainer = URL + 2D Illustrator/Mixed Media, 1–3 min, confident voice; education = Whiteboard Doodle or 3D Papercraft, 3–5 min; repurposing articles via URL. Preset choice is the most consequential decision, then duration, then voice. Settings apply to every scene (unlike Cinema Studio's per-shot settings).
