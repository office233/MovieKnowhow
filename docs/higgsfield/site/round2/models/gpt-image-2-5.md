# GPT Image 2.5 (Flare and Sunburst)

Sources:
- https://higgsfield.ai/gpt-image-2.5

Blog coverage of the launch: [../../blog/gpt-image-2-5-higgsfield.md](../../blog/gpt-image-2-5-higgsfield.md).

- Two modes: **Flare** = faster, for everyday images, social content, concepts and quick variations; **Sunburst** = slower, more editing precision for careful product work and campaign visuals.
- Editing: upload an image and describe the change, and **state what must stay** (subject, lighting, background). Keep each edit request to one clear change.
- Reference compositing: "Place her from the original photo into the location..." pattern - two inputs (person + location), then say what to keep (outfit, pose, expression) and add one lighting cue.
- Prompt pattern used by every gallery example: **shot type / camera look + light** first ("Candid phone-camera look, soft late-afternoon light:"), then subject with concrete wardrobe details, then props/action, then background. Short, comma-separated, very specific colours and materials.
- Cost depends on model and settings; check the generator.
