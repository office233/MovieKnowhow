## Notes

**Topic:** studio pipeline for client AI video (Aug 17 2026). No prompts.

**Problems with scattered tools:** character/style drift, assets in personal accounts, no per-deliverable cost visibility, many logins/credit systems/export formats.
**Standard pipeline (owner -> tool -> output):**
1. Script + shot breakdown (creative lead, Project Brief) — each shot with scene, characters, camera intent; the backbone doc.
2. Character setup (AI artist, **Soul ID** on 20-80 photos).
3. References (AI artist, **Elements**; Cinema Studio 4.0 takes up to 50 refs) — wardrobe, props, locations, style.
4. Generation (AI director, **Cinema Studio 4.0**; several takes per scene; camera body, 5 lenses, aperture, 30+ moves, 7 genres, era, tempo, 50+ palettes; up to 30 s, 1080p).
5. Client formats (Marketing Studio: 9 ad formats incl. UGC, Tutorial, Unboxing, Product Review, TV Spot, up to 15 s; DTC Ads for statics).
6. Review/approval (shared project; Enterprise asset-level comments).
7. Post/delivery (Premiere/AE/DaVinci plugins; Audio tools + Lipsync Studio).
Supercomputer can replace steps 3-5 for batch work.

**Costing:** Marketing Studio 15 s = 89.2 cr (~$4.50 at 20 cr/$); approve on 3rd take ≈ 268 cr ($13.40); 10-clip campaign ≈ 2,680 cr. **Budget planned retakes** like a live shoot's takes; build references/character sheets first to cut retakes.
**Team plans:** Team (up to 9 members, 1,000 pooled cr/seat/month, 16 video + 16 image parallel, standard queue) vs Scale (up to 15, 2,500 cr/seat, 20 video + 24 image parallel, priority queue, SSO, admin spend control incl. pausing a member, detailed analytics). Enterprise: no seat cap, dedicated capacity + SLA, SOC 2, per-user caps, asset comments, **no-data-training clause**, legal indemnification. Commercial rights on all business plans.
**Integration levels:** editor plugins; MCP/CLI (credits only); API (separate credits; smaller model list). Supercomputer skills: Product UGC, Shorts Maker, faceless videos up to 15 min.
