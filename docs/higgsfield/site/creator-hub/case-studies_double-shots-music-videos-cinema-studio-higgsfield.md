# Double Shots Produces Music Videos for Pop Idol, Ivy, with Cinema Studio

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/case-studies/double-shots-music-videos-cinema-studio-higgsfield
Published: Sep 19, 2026 (10 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: customer story (Double Shots, Taipei, 14 people, ~100 campaigns/year). Music videos for singer 郁欣 Ivy ("Want Me Back", Apr 24 2026; second video June 2026), director Henry Tao. Tools: Nano Banana Pro (images), Seedance 2.0 (video), Cinema Studio, Topaz upscaling inside Higgsfield; Claude for prompt development.

**Numbers**: about US$1,500-2,000 of AI generation per music video; at least one full week of generation/refinement per video in post.

**Technique: plan AI shots into the shot list from day one**
1. Mark specific shots as AI-generated in the original shot list (impossible locations, weather, scale, animals, transformations) instead of using AI as a post-production rescue.
2. On shoot day, photograph the artist, wardrobe and key props specifically as source material for those AI shots so faces/costumes/objects match the live footage.
3. In post, explore several creative directions (image first, then video) before locking a look; cut chosen shots in with live action.
4. Finish selected outputs with upscaling.
- Example shots: owl approaching a period house; Ivy transforming into a panther (with an "IVY" diamond grill extending her identity); warehouse of metallic forms; birds over a monumental city at sunset.
- Shot lists are now generated with Claude and produced on Higgsfield instead of hand-drawn boards.

**Lesson**: for small-budget work AI is not always cheaper than shooting; the win is making "mega-budget" looking shots on a modest budget with the right idea. Pitch the idea first, not to the budget.
