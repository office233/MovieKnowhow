URL-to-ad: tool reads product page (name, pricing cues, features, images, brand colors, logo) -> script -> presenter/avatar video + VO -> formats.
**Higgsfield Click to Ad** (inside Marketing Studio): pulls name, description, **up to 8 photos**, brand colors, logo; review/swap assets -> pick template (10 presets) -> generate; clips up to 30 s; ~ $2 per 8 s 720p clip. Needs ≥1 clear product photo on the page; **product-only photos beat lifestyle shots with models**; best for physical goods with clear shapes (electronics, clothing, accessories, skincare, packaged food, home goods). Extends into Marketing Studio's 1,500+ templates in six categories (Product Shots, Ads, Marketplace, Posters, UGC Videos, Motion) and Cinema Studio; Soul ID keeps the spokesperson consistent.
Others: Creatify (~$3.30/20 s; fast angle testing, presenter face drifts), Pippit/CapCut (CapCut timeline + trending sounds + TikTok upload), AdCreative.ai (static+video, connects Google/Meta performance data, pre-spend scoring), InVideo & Pictory (stock-footage videos from URLs/blog posts), HeyGen (Avatar V from webcam clone, 175+ languages, ~20 credits/min; Creator $29/600 credits), Synthesia (100+ avatars, Digital Twin from 15 min recording, 30+ languages, no URL pipeline).
Higgsfield pricing quoted: Starter $15, Plus $49, Ultra $129/mo.
