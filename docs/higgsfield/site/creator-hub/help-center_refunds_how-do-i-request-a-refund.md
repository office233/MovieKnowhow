# How do I request a refund?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/refunds/how-do-i-request-a-refund
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (2 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (refunds). Eligibility: initial purchase + within 7 days (or longer where law requires; EEA/UK 14-day withdrawal mentioned elsewhere) + zero credits used. Refundable: initial subscription, Credit Packs, Auto-Refill charges, credit-type Exclusive Offers. Not refundable: renewals, access/feature offers once granted. Submit via contact page (email, date, amount, reason); processing up to 3 business days; fee up to 6%; subscription canceled immediately on approval. Terms of Use section 9.3.
