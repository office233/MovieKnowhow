# Can I get a partial refund for unused annual time?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/refunds/can-i-get-a-partial-refund-for-unused-annual-time
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (2 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (refunds). No prorated/partial refunds: all-or-nothing, only within 7 days (or local law) with zero credits used; service fee up to 6%. Outside the window: keep using the year, turn off auto-renewal, or downgrade at renewal.
