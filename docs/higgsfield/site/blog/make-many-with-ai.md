## Notes

**Title on page:** "Make Money With AI" (Mariam Barova, May 31 2026) — launching a product brand from one Supercomputer chat: 500K views, 833 waitlist signups, $121 total credits (incl. failed generations), 3 days, new account, zero ads.

**Workflow:**
1. **Let data pick the niche:** prompt Supercomputer to scan Amazon/Etsy/Shopify/Meta ads for a high-margin, content-friendly product (-> lipstick). It then mined thousands of Sephora/Amazon reviews for the recurring complaint ("dries your lips") -> positioning: half lipstick, half lip balm (squalane, shea, ceramides); it named the brand and wrote a manufacturer-ready spec sheet.
2. **Content engineered from performance data:** ask what content wins in the niche now; it analysed top beauty content of the last 30 days and produced five formats: ASMR close-ups, makeup application, GRWM, UGC unboxing, talking "gossip" format.
3. **Waitlist landing page** (validate before buying inventory), mobile-optimised, in brand style, auto-deployed from the same chat.
4. **Funnel:** IG/TikTok content -> bio link -> waitlist -> email list for launch. Est. 10% conversion -> ~$738 net from the first wave.
