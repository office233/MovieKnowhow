# Do my top-up credits expire, and what happens if I cancel?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/credits/top-up-credits-expire
Published: Aug 3, 2026 (2 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (credits). Expiry/cancel matrix: subscription credits usable until paid period ends, then gone; Credit Pack, Auto-Refill and Boost credits last 90 days from purchase and stay usable on the free plan (smaller model lineup); promo credits keep their own date. Tip: to keep using leftover credits on the full lineup while paying less, downgrade to the lowest plan instead of canceling.
