How to use: upload one still, pick a preset, generate - no prompt needed. "Mix" presets combine two motions with individual strengths (e.g. Turning Metal 0.85 + Melting 0.6), which is how the site builds compound effects. Higgsfield DOP presets run at 20 steps, 81 frames, strength 1, guide scale 6.
