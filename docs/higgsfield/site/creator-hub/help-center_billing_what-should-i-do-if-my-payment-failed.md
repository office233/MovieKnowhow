# What should I do if my payment failed?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/billing/what-should-i-do-if-my-payment-failed
Published: Aug 1, 2026 (3 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (billing). Fix order: complete 3D Secure -> check card details/funds -> other card or Apple Pay/bank -> incognito/other browser -> disable VPN -> ask bank to allow online/international payments. Failed renewal limits generations and Unlimited until paid; pay the unpaid invoice in All invoices or add a card and "Try to charge now". Failed-payment emails with a Complete payment button are legitimate (Stripe Checkout); Higgsfield never asks for card data by email.
