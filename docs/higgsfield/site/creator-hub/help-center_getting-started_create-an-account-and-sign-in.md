# How do I create an account and sign in?

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/getting-started/create-an-account-and-sign-in
Published: Jul 29, 2026 (2 min read)
Section: creator-hub (Higgsfield Creator Hub)

Type: help article (account basics). Sign up at higgsfield.ai with Google, Apple, Microsoft or email (6-digit verification code). 18+ only. Always sign in with the same method; password reset only for email accounts (Sign in -> Forgot password -> 6-digit code). Email cannot be changed; one email per account.
